From plan to live bot

Here is the whole journey in plain steps.

  1. Choose a planPick the plan on the pricing page. Buy with the email address you will register with.
  2. Create your Botters Lab accountOne email arrives after you buy. It has two links. The second one creates your account. Pick a username you like because it becomes part of your bots' web addresses.
  3. Build your botName it. Write what it should do in plain language. Add your files. Choose the model.
  4. Preview itChat with it as a visitor would. Try a question it should refuse. Nobody else sees it yet.
  5. Make it PublicConfirm the declarations. Press Make Public. Every bot is checked first.
  6. Share itCopy the share link or paste the embed into your own site.
  7. Sell accessPut a one-time price on it. Set a message cap and a top-up. Add a money-back guarantee if you want one. This starts on Novice.
  8. Watch it workSee conversations, boosts and revenue on your dashboard. Deeper analytics start on Expert.
