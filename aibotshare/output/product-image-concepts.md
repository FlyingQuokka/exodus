# Botterverse: ten product image concepts

Concepts only, so you can choose which are worth pursuing. Real app screenshots will replace the "screen" shots later, so these lean on illustration and staged scenes. Each has an image-generator prompt ready to use with the Exodus image engines.

House rules for every image:
- No text inside the image. AI image tools spell badly. Add headlines in the layout instead.
- No competitor or AI-provider logos. Show the four models as plain colour-coded chips.
- No invented earnings figures, income screens or fake testimonials.
- No real or realistic people. The founder is anonymous and a face invites questions. A bot character carries the personality.
- Brand scheme, taken from the app's Pleasant Pink theme and its hex values in the brief: page background #f8f7ff, sidebar and soft panels #ebedfc, cards #ffffff, brand pink #dc8fd5 (hover #c876c1, soft tint #ffe0fb), heading text #282637. Use the darker link pink #984f92 for small coloured text on a light page. Pink on a bright cyan background is only 1.25 to 1 contrast, so if cyan is used as an extra accent (it is the Nightshift accent, #00d1da) keep pink objects on white cards.

Shared style line to append to every prompt: *clean modern illustration, soft rounded shapes, generous white space, subtle shadows, friendly and calm, no text, no logos, no people.*

## 1. The bot with the key

**Shows:** A friendly bot character holding a single glowing key in front of a front door painted in the brand colour.
**Idea:** Ownership. The key is the thing GPT builders never held.
**Best for:** Home page hero and the "You never owned the link" section.
**Prompt:** A friendly round-headed robot character holding one large glowing key, standing in front of a plain rounded door, soft sky-blue background, warm pink and violet accents.

## 2. Three ways to sell

**Shows:** Three objects side by side: a ticket, a stack of identical copies and a parcel being handed across.
**Idea:** Sell access, Multi-sell and Transfer in one glance.
**Best for:** The three-ways section and the social share image.
**Prompt:** Three simple illustrated objects in a row on a clean background: a golden entry ticket, a neat stack of identical small robot cubes and a gift parcel passing from one pair of hands to another, consistent colour palette.

## 3. The Bot Market shelves

**Shows:** A market stall with shelves of small bot "cartridges", each with a price tag shape and a star shape. No numbers.
**Idea:** A place where Botters trade, not a public store for browsing.
**Best for:** Bot Market explainer and the Pro upgrade prompt.
**Prompt:** A cosy market stall with a striped awning and wooden shelves holding small rounded robot cartridges with blank price tags and little star ratings, bright sky-blue and white stripes.

## 4. The sales page with a helper

**Shows:** A laptop showing a generic sales page layout in blocks, with a chat bubble in the corner and a bot answering a question.
**Idea:** The embeddable FAQ bot answering before a prospect leaves.
**Best for:** The embed section until the live demo is ready.
**Prompt:** A laptop on a desk showing an abstract sales page made of grey and colour blocks, a small chat bubble in the lower corner with a tiny robot avatar, soft light, tidy workspace.

## 5. Old link, new home

**Shows:** A broken chain link on the left turning into a connected link on the right that leads to a bot page.
**Idea:** The problem-to-solution turn. A dead link becomes a link you own.
**Best for:** The problem section and the launch emails.
**Prompt:** A broken chain link fading to grey on the left, a bright connected chain link on the right leading to a small bot page card, left side muted and right side vivid.

## 6. Four models, one dial

**Shows:** A rotary selector with four colour-coded chips feeding into one bot.
**Idea:** You choose the model for each bot.
**Best for:** The "Pick your favourite model" section.
**Prompt:** A round selector dial with four plain colour-coded chips around it, a thin line running from the chosen chip into a friendly robot, no logos on the chips.

## 7. The importer

**Shows:** A sheet of written instructions and a few file icons flowing through a funnel into a bot silhouette.
**Idea:** Your old GPT moves across without starting again.
**Best for:** The importer section and a "move your GPT" email header.
**Prompt:** A sheet of paper with blank text lines and three small file icons sliding into a wide funnel that pours into the outline of a robot, soft gradient background.

## 8. The trust seal

**Shows:** A bot page card with a round certification seal and a short checklist of tick marks.
**Idea:** Certified by Botterverse means genuine and declared, not "guaranteed safe".
**Best for:** The trust section and the Certified by Botterverse page.
**Prompt:** A clean card showing a small robot avatar, a round seal badge with a tick and a column of seven blank checklist rows with small tick marks, calm blue and white palette.

## 9. The Botter's Lab

**Shows:** An isometric workshop where small bots are assembled on a bench, with shelves of finished bots.
**Idea:** The member area as a place with character.
**Best for:** The about page, member welcome emails and the affiliate page.
**Prompt:** An isometric illustration of a bright workshop with a long bench where small friendly robots are being assembled, shelves of finished robots behind, pink and violet lighting.

## 10. The Founders badge

**Shows:** A bot card with a gold ribbon badge pinned to its corner and a soft glow.
**Idea:** A status marker the Founder earns once and keeps.
**Best for:** The Founders offer section and every founders email.
**Prompt:** A bot profile card with a small robot avatar and a gold rosette badge pinned at the top corner, a soft golden glow, clean background, celebratory but calm.

---

## My picks

If you want to start small: **1** for the hero, **2** for the selling section and **10** for the Founders offer. Those three cover the page's main promise, its main mechanism and its main offer.
