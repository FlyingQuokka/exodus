About

Built for Botters

AI Bot Share is where Botters build AI bots, choose their own model and sell them three ways for one published fee.

Why this exists

On 16 August 2026 OpenAI closed new GPT creation to personal accounts. The GPT Store had never published a payout formula. Every GPT lived behind OpenAI's login under OpenAI's branding. Removal came with under eight hours' notice. A whole market of imers needed somewhere to build and sell bots on terms they could read.

What we stand for

  • Published fees. Every sale pays one fee: 5% or 3% of the price after tax, plus 30 cents.
  • Your choice of model. Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or Gemini for each bot, never routed for you.
  • Real ways to sell. Access, resale rights or outright sale, in language imers already use.
  • Plain terms. Cancel any time and keep access to the end of the billing period.
The Bot Market

Three ways to turn one bot into income

Pick one or list a bot under all three. Selling in the Bot Market starts at Pro.

Sell access

Sell it outright
