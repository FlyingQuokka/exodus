# Botterverse marketing brief for Exodus and Genesis

Written 18 October 2026 from the product's own specification and build records. Everything here is a fact from those documents or is listed as an open question in section 14. If a fact is not here do not invent it. Ask the owner.

How to read the tags. **[beta]** means the feature is built during the closed beta and is not there on day one. **[R2]** means Release 2 (the main launch) and is not in the closed beta. A feature with no tag is built. Section 10 has the full status.

---

## 1. The product

**In one paragraph.** Botterverse is a platform where people build, share and sell AI chatbots. A member (a "botter") builds a bot in the member area (Botter's Lab) by writing instructions and adding their own files. They pick an AI model then share the bot by link or embed it on their own site. They can charge for access and list the bot on Bot Market so other members can buy it. Members who bring in new members earn ongoing commission through the affiliate programme. Every bot is checked before it can go Public because the idea behind the platform is AI for good.

**In one sentence.** Botterverse lets you build an AI chatbot then share it or sell it and earn from the members you bring in.

---

## 2. The problem and the promise

**The problem.** From 16 August 2026 OpenAI stopped Free and paid personal ChatGPT accounts (Free, Go, Plus and Pro) from creating or publishing new custom GPTs. It replaced GPTs with enterprise-focused Workspace Agents. Internet marketers who had built income by creating and selling custom GPTs lost that ability. The complaints about GPTs were already consistent:

- no real revenue sharing and opaque payouts
- no ownership, with OpenAI's branding and no analytics on your own users
- no way to contact your users
- poor discovery
- a sudden removal with very little notice

**The promise.** Botterverse is built so a builder is not left at the mercy of one company. Each line below is carried by a feature:

| Complaint about GPTs | What Botterverse offers |
|---|---|
| Months of work suddenly blocked | An importer that moves an existing bot's instructions and files in |
| No ownership | Export of your bots and your data **[beta]** |
| Dependent on one company | Four AI model families to choose from |
| No visibility into your users | Analytics on every bot |
| Carries someone else's branding | Your own bot style and a removable powered-by tag from the Pro plan |
| Opaque money | A published fee for each plan and a clear earnings and payout screen |

Do not write "we will never pull the rug" as a promise of permanence. Say what the benefits of the features are. The spec plans a dated public charter (notice periods and the export guarantee) for the marketing site but it is not written yet (section 14).

## 3. Who it is for

### Initial market: the closed beta group

A small group of internet marketers (IMers). Release 1 is for them to judge whether to recommend Botterverse to their own lists. The affiliate programme with ongoing commission is a key attraction for them.

- **What they want:** a replacement for the custom GPTs they sold plus a way to sell bots to buyers and to each other. They also want recurring affiliate income from products they promote.
- **Their words, as the product documents record them:** IMers, GPTs, WSO, PLR, WarriorPlus, JVZoo, launches, JV pages, affiliates, affiliate contests and "my list".
- **What they distrust:** vendors who disappear and platforms that change the rules overnight. Say plainly what is built and what is coming.
- **Where they shop:** WarriorPlus and JVZoo style launches. That is also the planned route for the main launch (Release 2).

### Wider audiences the documents name

| Audience | What they want | Where they enter |
|---|---|---|
| Bot builders who lost or fear losing a platform | A safe home they own and can leave with their data | Apprentice at $5 a month or Novice at $47 |
| Sellers who want to charge for a bot | A one-time paywall and a way to be paid | Novice and above |
| Creators who trade bots in the PLR style | To flip or resell a bot and build a reputation | Bot Market selling from Pro |
| Deep-pocketed solo marketers | A/B testing and advanced analytics | Master |
| Buyers of another member's bot | A working bot at a one-time price | Bot Market buying from Apprentice |
| A seller's own customers | To use the bot the seller built | The seller's page, where they are invited to become botters |

There is deliberately **no public store for end customers to browse**. The first GPT Store failed partly for that reason. Bot Market is for members trading with each other. Distribution is by link, embed and affiliate promotion. Do not describe Botterverse as an app store or a place to browse bots for personal use.

## 4. Features and benefits

Each line gives the feature then the benefit to the member.

### Build a bot

- **One-page bot builder.** Instructions, knowledge files, starters, model, category, integrations, pricing and sharing settings sit on one page. Benefit: you set up and run a bot from one place.
- **Preview chat inside the builder.** Benefit: you test the bot the way a visitor will meet it before you publish.
- **Personal and Public mode.** A new bot is Personal and private to you. Public opens sharing, embedding and selling. Benefit: you build in private then publish when ready.
- **Duplicate, pause and delete.** Benefit: you can copy a bot that works or take one out of service.
- **A bot limit for each plan.** One bot on Apprentice, twenty on Novice, fifty on Expert and unlimited from Pro. Benefit: you pay for the size you need.
- **Edited text is saved when you press Save.** Instructions and settings never change on a keypress. Benefit: no half-finished edit reaches your visitors.

### Knowledge and instructions

- **Your own instructions.** Benefit: the bot behaves the way you decide.
- **Knowledge files.** You upload PDF, Word, text, Markdown or CSV files (limits apply to the number and size of files). Benefit: the bot answers from your own material.
- **Large knowledge is searched.** When a bot has more material than fits in one reply it sends the best matching passages. Benefit: big libraries still work.
- **Conversation starters.** Benefit: visitors know what to ask first.
- **Long conversations are summarised and not cut off.** Benefit: a bot keeps the thread of a long chat.
- **A file that cannot be read is flagged** and you are asked to try another copy. Benefit: you are never left guessing.

### Chat and models

- **Four model families.** Claude (the default), GPT, Gemini and Grok. Benefit: you are not tied to one company and can pick a model for each bot. Apprentice members can use the everyday models but not each provider's most expensive one.
- **Integrations (Novice and above).** A simple form (name, address, method, parameters and key type) lets a bot call another service. The mockups show "Create CRM contact" and "Book discovery call". Benefit: a bot can do things as well as talk.
- **Credits are used by the real size of each message.** Benefit: the cost matches what was used.
- **Limits protect your credits.** Every visitor is rate limited and each bot has a daily credit ceiling. Benefit: a busy launch cannot empty your balance without warning.
- **A bot that cannot answer tells the visitor why** in plain fixed messages. Benefit: visitors are never left with a blank screen.
- **An AI disclosure line** is added on embeds and white-labelled pages. Benefit: visitors know they are talking to an AI.

### Share and embed

- **Your own page for each public bot** on your own subdomain (your username before botterverse.com). Your username is chosen at sign-up and cannot be changed. Benefit: a clean link you can share.
- **Embed on your own site.** A copied script tag shows a free bot that needs no sign-up inside your page. Any other bot embeds as a "Chat with (bot name)" button that opens its own page. Benefit: your bot lives where your audience already is.
- **Paywall (Novice and above).** One-time purchase with an optional usage cap and optional message top-ups. Benefit: you charge once and protect yourself from open-ended credit costs.
- **A money-back guarantee you choose.** None, 7 days or 14 days, with fixed wording. Benefit: a promise buyers can read and you can honour.
- **Star ratings from paying users.** Benefit: social proof on your paywall page.
- **Bot style (Pro and above).** Logo, display name, five colours, font, size, corner style and switches for the header, avatar and powered-by tag, with a live preview. Benefit: the bot looks like your brand.
- **Powered-by tag.** It carries your own referral link so an end customer who joins earns you commission. Pro and above can remove it. Benefit: lower plans earn from it too.
- **Require visitors to sign up (a switch).** Benefit: you choose between friction-free chat and a list of known users.

### Bot Market: buying and selling

- **A catalogue of bots other members list.** Search, filters (category, model, integrations, sale type, owner and release date), a "4 stars and up" filter and sorting. Benefit: buyers find what they need.
- **Buying from Apprentice.** Benefit: the widest pool of buyers for a seller.
- **Selling from Pro.** Benefit: listings come from invested members.
- **Two ways to sell.** Transfer (one sale and the bot moves to the buyer) or Multi-sell (a copy for each buyer while you keep the original). Benefit: sell a bot once at a high price or many times at a lower one.
- **Four licence types.** Personal use, Resell rights, Master resell rights and PLR. Benefit: the PLR-style rights this audience already knows are enforced by the app.
- **Reviews with a "Verified purchase" label and seller replies.** Benefit: reputation that cannot be exported.
- **Seller profile and badges.** Four badges at launch: Founder, Verified Seller, 10 Sales and Top Rated. Benefit: trust signals for buyers.
- **Platform-verified history on a listing.** Sales and conversation counts show by default. Revenue shows only if the seller opts in. Benefit: a real track record.
- **Recommendations.** "Customers who also bought", "You might also like" and "More from this seller". Benefit: more sales without extra work.
- **Per-listing affiliate links.** A seller can offer a percentage to members who promote the listing. Benefit: other people sell for you.
- **A shareable listing page.** Benefit: you promote your listing to your own list as well as in the catalogue.
- **Price guidance.** Category averages for price, messages per buyer and conversion. Benefit: you price with data.

### Analytics

- **Dashboard.** Credit balance, revenue over time, active bots and usage this month. Benefit: one glance shows how things stand.
- **Bot analytics by plan.** Basic (Novice and above) shows conversations, messages, credits used and revenue with a volume graph. Deeper (Expert and above) adds busiest hours, top conversation starters and category benchmarks. Advanced (Master and above) adds a conversion panel that sets views against purchases. Benefit: you see what works.
- **An info mark on every figure** explains what it means. Benefit: no guessing at a number.
- **Conversation insights [beta].** An optional run lists questions the bot could not answer, common topics and suggested additions. It uses credits and is off by default. Benefit: the bot improves from real use.

### A/B testing (Master Tier)

- **Test two or more versions of a bot's instructions and starters** on real conversations. Price is never tested. Benefit: you learn which version converts better.
- **A fair split.** The same visitor always sees the same version. Benefit: results you can trust.
- **A significance test** with a "not enough data yet" message. Benefit: no false winners.
- **Choose a winner** with one action. Benefit: the better version becomes the bot.

### Importing

- **Importer wizard.** You paste your existing instructions, upload files, add integrations, review and create the bot. AI suggests a name, description, category and starters. Benefit: you move an existing bot's setup in without starting again. The importer does not connect to an OpenAI account. You paste the content in.
- **Botterverse pays for the importer's AI calls** and for listing suggestions. Benefit: no credits are used.

### Credits and billing

- **Included credits every month** with your plan, plus credit packs and optional auto top-up. Benefit: you do not run dry in the middle of a launch.
- **Promo codes** that add bonus credits. Benefit: easy to give credits away.
- **Monthly or annual billing** with plan changes up or down through a prorated checkout. Benefit: you can move as you grow.
- **Cancel with one click and keep your plan to the end of the period you paid for.** Benefit: no lock-in.
- **Data export [beta].** Benefit: your bots and conversations can leave with you. A member requests it on Settings and downloads a zip when it is ready. It holds:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Account | the username, name, email address, country, timezone, plan with its dates and email preferences (being added during the beta) |
| Bots | each bot and its settings: name, description, instructions, starters, model, category, licence, paywall, style and integrations (never a password or key) |
| Knowledge files | each bot's uploaded files, as they were uploaded |
| Instruction versions | each bot's saved instruction history |
| Conversations with my bots | the chats visitors and customers had with the member's bots |
| My conversations as a customer | the member's own chats with other members' bots, as the chat showed them |
| Sales credited to me | each sale and what it paid the member |
| My purchases | plans, credit packs, bot access and Bot Market purchases |
| My commissions | affiliate commissions |
| Credit ledger and earnings ledger | every credit and earnings movement |
| Avatar and bot style logos | the member's own images |

Left out on purpose: the hidden contents of a bought bot while its refund window is open and of bots whose licence hides them, the time before a Transferred bot reached the member, integration keys and passwords, staff notes and a list of customers' email addresses (planned for a later release). Say "your bots, knowledge and conversations go with you". Do not say "everything" or "all your customers".

### The affiliate programme

See section 7. Benefit: ongoing commission on the members you refer.

### Trust and safety

See section 8. Benefit: a platform that buyers and partners can trust.

### Themes

## 6. Selling with a paywall and on Bot Market

**How a member sells**

1. **Paywall sale (Novice and above).** The member switches a Public bot to paid. They set a one-time price (at least $5 by default), an optional message cap, an optional top-up size and price (at least $1) and a guarantee of none, 7 or 14 days. Buyers pay through Stripe Checkout and need an account.
2. **Bot Market sale (Pro and above).** The member lists a Public bot with a price and description. They choose Transfer or Multi-sell, a licence type and an optional affiliate percentage. The listing is scanned first and goes live if it is clear. The member needs saved licence terms of their own before listing.

**Fees**

- The platform fee is **5% plus 30 cents per sale** below Pro and **3% plus 30 cents** from Pro. It covers paywall sales, message top-ups and Bot Market sales. The fee is never more than the sale.
- Illustration only (fee arithmetic and not an earnings claim): a $49 paywall sale on Novice carries a fee of $2.45 plus $0.30 so the seller's earnings are $46.25.
- A listing affiliate percentage, if the seller sets one, is taken from what remains after the platform fee.
- Withdrawals carry payout fees passed on at cost. The member sees the fee and the amount that will arrive before confirming.

**Earnings and payouts**

- Sales build up as **earnings** in the member's Botterverse account. The words "balance" and "wallet" are not used.
- Earnings from a sale can be withdrawn after a **holdback** of 21 days by default. A refund inside that period needs no money to move.
- The minimum withdrawal is **$50** by default. Requests are sent as soon as they are made unless payout days are set.
- Payout set-up happens at the first withdrawal. A member can build and sell with no payout account. They enter bank details once on Stripe's own hosted form. Botterverse never sees them.
- Botterverse pays through Stripe Global Payouts to more than 160 countries in the local currency. UK payouts can be sent automatically. Payouts abroad are handled by hand by the Botter Support Team until Stripe widens access. Do not promise speed.
- A member in a country that cannot be paid can still register, build, buy and publish free bots. Earnings build up until payout becomes possible.
- Botterverse is the seller of record and takes the payment. The seller's share is a royalty owed to them.

**Refunds and guarantees**

- **Paywall sale:** the buyer agrees that access starts at once and that they lose the right to cancel except under the seller's guarantee. The seller can refund within the **refund period** (14 days by default) and the money comes from their earnings.
- **Guarantee claim:** if the seller chose a guarantee the buyer claims through a form. The seller has three days by default to refund or ask staff to review. If they do neither the claim is refunded automatically from the seller's earnings.
- **Bot Market sale:** staff refund only if the bot does not work or is not as described. A buyer who simply started using a bot has no right to a refund. An approved refund is less a processing fee equal to the platform fee. Putting the bot on sale ends the right to a refund.
- **Chargebacks:** a lost chargeback is treated as a refund and dispute fees come from the seller's earnings.
- **When a bot is taken away** (a ban, an upheld licence breach, an account closure or a deletion) customers with unused messages are refunded from the seller's earnings.
- **Platform sales** (subscriptions and credit packs) are refunded at staff discretion. No money-back guarantee is stated (section 14).

## 7. The affiliate programme

**What it is.** Ongoing commission on the Botterverse subscription payments of the members a person refers. It is flat and single level. There are no multi-level commissions.

**How a member joins.** From the Affiliates page in Botter's Lab by pressing Join. It needs a plan from **Novice** up. Apprentice shows an upgrade prompt. Only an active member can join. Staff do not add affiliates by hand.

**What earns commission**

1. **Subscription payments by members they referred**, meaning the first payment and every renewal. An annual payment earns on the larger payment in the same way as a monthly one.
2. **Bot Market sales through a listing link**, at a percentage the seller set for that listing.

Nothing else earns commission. **Credit packs earn none.**

**Rates (settings that can change)**

- **50%** of a referred member's first ever payment, whatever the plan.
- **20%** of every later payment. That includes renewals and the first payment after an upgrade or a downgrade.

Worked on listed monthly prices with no discount (illustration only and not an earnings claim):

| Referred member's plan | First payment (50%) | Each later payment (20%) |
|---|---|---|
| Apprentice $5 | $2.50 | $1.00 |
| Novice $47 | $23.50 | $9.40 |
| Expert $97 | $48.50 | $19.40 |
| Pro $197 | $98.50 | $39.40 |
| Master $297 | $148.50 | $59.40 |

**When it is paid.** A commission is payable only after the refund period (14 days by default) has passed since the payment, so a refunded signup never pays. The application works out each amount from the referred member's payment history. Staff review each batch and press a Pay commissions button. The commission is then added to the member's earnings as available to withdraw. Commission on a payment that is later refunded or lost to a chargeback is taken back.

**How to withdraw.** Exactly as sellers do. The minimum is $50 by default and payout fees are passed on. Payout needs a country Stripe can pay. Members in the EU cannot pay for any plan so none can be affiliates.

**How referral links work.** A member shares the link on their Affiliates page. It ends in their own username. When someone clicks it they are sent to the Botterverse marketing site with the member's referral identity attached. The affiliate software records who referred whom. When the referred person pays the application checks the payment history and works out the commission. A member only ever sees their own username in the link. A seller's powered-by tag uses the same kind of link. A Bot Market listing link adds the promoter's username to the listing's address. That click counts for 30 days by default and the last click wins.

**Rules**

- **Self-referral is a breach.** Referring yourself through your own account, another account of yours or your own card is checked. A repeat offender is banned and their earnings are forfeited.
- The programme's own sign-up form is switched off. Members join only inside Botter's Lab.

**In the closed beta.** The programme is visible and members can join and share links. The commission import, review and payment steps are still being built **[beta]**. Do not say commission is already being paid until the owner confirms.

## 8. Trust and safety and the AI for good promise

**The idea.** Botterverse is for good bots. Members are guided to create only good bots and bad bots are not tolerated.

**Not allowed (categories only).** A bot cannot go Public if it is built for:

- sexual content
- gambling and betting
- illegal activity, including drugs, weapons and counterfeit goods
- fraud, scams and malicious software
- harm to children or to anyone else
- hate and harassment
- impersonating a real person or organisation
- misleading financial or health claims, such as guaranteed returns, miracle cures and share tips
- covert monitoring of individuals
- spam, fake reviews and political manipulation
- passing off work as someone's own for academic credit

Staff hold the list and can tighten it without a release. Do not publish a longer list or any example prompts. Say "the full list is in the terms".

**Declarations.** Every Public bot publishes seven honest answers on its Certified by Botterverse page:

1. Does it collect personal details?
2. Does it handle sensitive data such as health or beliefs?
3. Does it handle financial or payment details?
4. Is it meant for under-18s or likely to be used by them?
5. Does it give advice in a regulated area (medical, legal, financial or insurance)?
6. Does it make automated decisions that affect a person?
7. Does it send data to anyone beyond the AI model provider?

A declaration is disclosed and not banned. The scan fills in what it finds and locks it. A seller who disagrees can ask the Botter Support Team and staff can unlock one declaration on one bot. A false declaration has consequences under the terms.

**The scan.** A bot is scanned before it can go Public and again before it is listed. Every saved change to a Public bot runs the scan again. A small sample of live conversations is also screened after the fact and never interrupts a customer. A bot that matches a blocked category cannot go Public or goes back to Personal. A bot in a review category stays Public while staff look at it.

**What members can expect**

- A **Certified by Botterverse** badge on every bot that cannot be switched off. It shows the bot is genuine and what the owner declared. **It does not certify quality or safety.** Never say it does.
- A **Report this bot** control on every public bot page, embed and listing. No account is needed.
- One moderation queue where staff handle reports, scans and reviews.
- Protection for the hidden instructions of bots that were bought.
- Final bans for serious breaches, with earnings forfeited.
- Legal documents (terms, privacy policy and licence template) are drafts until a UK data protection solicitor has reviewed them.

## 12. FAQ

**1. Do I need to code?**
No. You write instructions in plain language and upload files. Integrations use a simple form.

**2. Which AI models can I use?**
Claude (the default), GPT, Gemini and Grok. The models on offer can change. A bot already using a model keeps it when that model is withdrawn for new bots. Apprentice members can use everyday models only.

**3. Is there a free plan?**
No. Apprentice is $5 a month ($50 a year) and there is no free trial. A visitor can still chat with another member's free Public bot without paying.

**4. What are credits?**
Credits pay for the messages a bot sends. You pay for your own testing and for the chats your visitors have with your bots. Your plan includes credits each month and they expire at renewal. Credits you buy never expire.

**5. Can I sell my bots?**
Yes. Charge for access with a one-time paywall from Novice. List a bot for sale on Bot Market from Pro as a Transfer or a Multi-sell with one of four licences.

**6. What does Botterverse take?**
5% plus 30 cents per sale below Pro and 3% plus 30 cents from Pro. These are settings and can change.

**7. How do I withdraw earnings?**
Earnings can be withdrawn after a holdback (21 days by default) and the minimum is $50 by default. You set up payout once on Stripe's own form. Payout fees are shown before you confirm. Some countries cannot be paid.

**8. How does the affiliate programme work?**
Join from the Affiliates page on Novice or above and share your link. You earn 50% of a referred member's first payment and 20% of each later payment. The rates are settings. Credit packs earn nothing. Commission is payable after the refund period and the payment steps are being finished during the beta.

**9. Can I move my existing GPT?**
You can paste its instructions into the importer and upload its files. Botterverse does not connect to your OpenAI account.

**10. Can I use Botterverse from the EU?**
You can use free Public bots. Nobody in the EU can pay for a plan or credits or buy a bot.

**11. What is not allowed?**
Bots for sexual content, gambling, illegal activity, scams, harm to others and similar. Bots are scanned and reported bots are reviewed. A breach can lead to removal or a ban.

**12. Can I cancel?**
Yes with one click. You keep your plan to the end of the period you paid for. An annual plan ends at the end of the current month and the whole unused months are refunded. Closing an account is not available in the closed beta.

## 13. Glossary

- **Bot:** an AI chatbot a member builds with instructions and files.
- **Botter:** a member who builds bots. **Sharer** and **Bottie** are badge family names.
- **Botter's Lab:** the member area.
- **Botter Support Team:** the people who help members and send some payouts by hand.
- **Plan or tier:** Apprentice, Novice, Expert, Pro or Master. Shown with "Botter" after the name.
- **Credits:** what messages cost. Included credits come with a plan. Bought credits never expire.
- **Credit pack:** credits bought in one go at the cart checkout.
- **Auto top-up:** a charge that adds credits when your credits are low. It is off until you switch it on.
- **Promo code:** a code that adds bonus credits. It never discounts a price.
- **Personal and Public:** Personal means private to the owner. Public means shareable and sellable.
- **Paywall:** a one-time charge for access to a bot.
- **Access:** a buyer's right to chat with a paid bot. It can carry a message cap.
- **Message top-up:** extra messages a buyer pays for after a cap is reached.
- **Guarantee:** a seller's money-back promise of none, 7 or 14 days.
- **Knowledge files:** files a bot answers from.
- **Starters:** suggested first messages.
- **Integration:** a link to another service that a bot can call.
- **Importer:** the wizard that builds a bot from pasted instructions and files.
- **Bot style:** the look of a seller's bot pages (Pro and above).
- **Powered-by tag:** a small Botterverse link on bot pages that carries the seller's referral link.
- **Bot Market:** the marketplace where members trade bots.
- **Listing:** a bot offered for sale on Bot Market.
- **Transfer:** a single sale where the bot moves to the buyer.
- **Multi-sell:** a sale where each buyer receives a copy and the seller keeps the original.
- **Licence types:** Personal use, Resell rights, Master resell rights and PLR.
- **PLR:** private label rights. The holder can rebrand and resell.
- **Platform fee:** the percentage plus fixed amount Botterverse takes from a sale.
- **Earnings:** a seller's or affiliate's share of sales that is waiting to be withdrawn.
- **Holdback:** the wait before earnings from a sale can be withdrawn.
- **Withdrawal:** a request to be paid out. The minimum is $50 by default.
- **Declaration:** one of seven disclosures about a bot.
- **Scan:** the automatic check before a bot goes Public or is listed.
- **Certified by Botterverse:** the badge and page that show what a bot's owner declared.
- **A/B test:** a test of two versions of a bot (Master and above).
- **Insights:** an optional report of what a bot could not answer.
- **Badges:** Founder, Verified Seller, 10 Sales and Top Rated.
- **Founders coupon:** a coupon for 50% off a subscription for life. It earns the Founder badge.
- **Affiliate, referral and commission:** the member who refers, the person referred and the money earned.
- **Listing affiliate:** a member who promotes another seller's listing for a share the seller sets.
- **Release 1 and Release 2:** the closed beta build and the main launch build.
