Questions people usually ask first

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe what the bot should do in plain language, add your files and choose a model.

Which AI models can I use?

Claude, GPT, Gemini or Grok. Apprentice uses everyday models and every other plan includes every model.

How do visitors reach my bot?

Send them a link or paste one snippet into your own site. Visitors are told they are talking to an AI before the first reply.

Can I charge people to use my bot?

Yes. Set a one-time price for access and a message cap with a top-up. Selling with a paywall starts on the Novice plan. You can add a money-back guarantee on your own bot if you want one.

What does Botterverse charge when I sell?

The selling fee is 5% + 30 cents per sale on Novice and Expert. It is 3% + 30 cents per sale on Pro and Master.

What is the difference between the plans?

Plans differ in the number of bots, monthly credits, safety scans and features. Expert adds deeper analytics. Pro adds your own look and Bot Market. Master adds A/B tests.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Plans are monthly or annual. Annual costs ten times the monthly price. Every plan has a 30-day money-back guarantee.
