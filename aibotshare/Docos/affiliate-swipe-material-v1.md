Ready-made emails, posts, pitches and banner texts for affiliates. Copy them and make them your own.

Lines in [square brackets] are for you to fill in or delete. Nothing here has been built or loaded into the affiliate platform.

## 1. How to use this

1. Pick an item. Change the words so they sound like you.
2. Replace `[YOUR AFFILIATE LINK]` with your own link. You will find it under Affiliates in the Botters Lab.
3. Keep the disclosure line in every item. Do not remove it. Move it if you must but it has to be easy to see.
4. Say only what the item says. If you are unsure about a feature check the Help centre first.

### The rules

- No income claims. No results claims. No earnings promises. Never say or hint that anyone will earn.
- No invented stories or testimonials. Write only about what you have seen yourself.
- Do not name a competitor.
- Do not mention any badge shown in the members area.
- Say "affiliate link" or "I earn a commission if you join through my link" in every item. Each item below includes a disclosure line.
- Mention commission rates only as "at launch" and only if you must. These items leave them out.
- Do not quote prices. The only price wording you may use is "Plans from $5 a month".
- Do not call any plan free. There is no free plan and no trial.
- Selling access starts on Novice Botter. Selling on Bot Market starts on Pro Botter. Do not imply a plan can do more than it can.
- A bot is only as good as its instructions and files. Do not promise sales or customers or income.
- Botter Boosters earn no commission. Do not push them.
- To promote a Bot Market listing use the link from Promote on that listing page. Use your own link for everything else.

## 6. Words to avoid

Keep this page beside you when you write.

**About money**
- "Free" used for any plan. There is no free plan and no trial.
- "Passive income", "make money", "easy money" and "side income".
- "Earn" used to promise anything to a reader.
- "Guaranteed", "risk-free", "proven" and "foolproof".
- Any figure that sounds like a result. Any example of what someone made.
- Any price other than "Plans from $5 a month".
- Commission rates stated as fixed. If you mention them say "at launch".

**About the product**
- "Unlimited" unless you are talking about the bot count on Pro and above.
- "Instant", "overnight" and "in minutes". The guide gives no times.
- "Your customers" for the customer download. Say the customers who ticked the box to share their details.
- "Secret" or "unbreakable" or "copy-proof" about instructions and files. No instruction is a perfect lock.
- "Certified" as proof of quality. The Certified page shows who owns a bot and what the owner declared. It does not vouch for the answers.
- "Always right" or "never wrong" about a bot.
- "Sell" without saying which plan. Selling access starts on Novice. Selling on Bot Market starts on Pro.
- "Everyone can use every model". The cheapest plan has the everyday models only.

**About people and other brands**
- Any named competitor or named AI product other than the four model families Claude, GPT, Gemini and Grok.
- Invented customers or reviews or quotes or stories.
- Any claim that someone will earn from referring others.
- Any badge shown in the members area.

**About style**
- Exclamation marks.
- Hype words such as "revolutionary", "game-changing" and "ultimate".
- Hashtags if the item says none.
- Hiding the disclosure.
