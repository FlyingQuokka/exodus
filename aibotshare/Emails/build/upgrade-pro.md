## UP04 - Choose your licence (day 5, Pro upgrade)

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] Four licences, one choice

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: last time we covered the listing form and I said one setting decides what a buyer may do with your bot.

New topic: that setting is the licence. You have four to choose from and your own terms sit alongside them.

**The four licences**

When you list a bot, you choose Personal use, Resell rights, Master resell rights or PLR. A bot you built yourself carries no licence until you list it, so any of the four is open to you.

The guide's table sets out what each one allows. These points help you choose:

1. Personal use - the buyer's copy stays Personal and they can't open it to customers. They can't see its instructions or knowledge files.
2. Resell rights - the buyer can sell copies on Personal use. They can't see the instructions or knowledge files either.
3. Master resell rights - the buyer can sell copies on Personal use or Resell rights. They can't see the instructions or knowledge files either.
4. PLR - the buyer can rename and edit the bot.

So if your instructions are the valuable part, keep them private with one of the first three. If you want buyers to make the bot their own, choose PLR.

**Your licence terms**

Open [Settings](https://lab.{{domain}}/settings) then Licence terms. Press Copy our template to start (this is a one-off copy) then edit it under Your licence terms. Press Save. The saved terms are checked. Botterverse doesn't give legal advice about them.

A listing can't be submitted until the terms are saved. When you change them later, press Notify my buyers. That emails every current licence holder a link to the terms page. Saving alone sends no email.

**A starting point**

For example, add two plain lines to the template:

> Support: I answer questions about this bot within [NUMBER] working days.
> Updates: later versions may be sold separately and are not included.

**Task for today**

Copy the template and add your own lines. Then press Save. Decide which licence suits the bot you want to sell.

See you soon,

Your Botter Support Team

P.S. Next up: buyers read more than the listing and they read about you.

Notify my buyers sends an email and saving alone sends none (6.7). No legal advice (6.7). Bought Personal use, Resell rights or Master resell rights bots hide Instructions and Knowledge files while a PLR bot can be renamed and edited (3.1). Personal-licence bots stay private (4.1).

## UP06 - Send leads home (day 9, Pro upgrade)

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] Customers into your own CRM

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: last time we looked at your seller profile and badges. I said we'd see how customers who agree to share their details reach your own CRM.

New topic: that's the CRM connector. It sends events to a web address you choose and it only ever sends details for customers who have ticked the box to share them.

**Consent first**

A box asks the customer to send their name and email address to you. It's unticked and never required. It shows your name and username with a link to your privacy notice. A customer sees it when they create an account from your bot's page, on the paywall or once in the chat. Customers without an account are never sent.

Because only customers who agreed are included, turn on Require visitors to sign up in Sharing and embed on the [Create a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/create) page for the bots that matter (each bot must be saved first). Visitors then have accounts and can be asked.

**Setting it up**

1. Open [Settings](https://lab.{{domain}}/settings) then CRM.
2. Enter your Webhook address. It must start with https and it comes from your CRM.
3. Enter Your privacy notice. This address is required and it's the one setting that must be right first, because customers follow it before they agree.
4. Press Save. Your signing secret appears once, so copy it somewhere safe. Your developer can use it to check each event (HMAC-SHA256 in the X-ABS-Signature header).
5. Press Send test event and check it arrives in your CRM.
6. Press Switch on.

Each event holds the event name, a tag, the time, the customer's name and email address and your bot's name and slug. Switch off, Replace secret and Disconnect sit on the same page. After repeated failures, the connection switches itself off and you're emailed.

You become responsible for this data under data protection law and you must honour every unsubscribe.

**Task for today**

Work through steps 1 to 5 then Send test event. Switch on only when the test event looks right.

Speak soon,

Your Botter Support Team

## UP07 - Tag the moments (day 11, Pro upgrade)

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] Which moments your CRM hears about

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: last time you connected your CRM and I said it can hear about moments as well as names. You choose which ones.

New topic: those are lifecycle tags. You set a tag name for each event and the connector sends it to your CRM when that event happens.

**How they work**

The events come in groups. Lead covers signing up to a bot, starting a first conversation and signing up without ever chatting. Engaged covers reaching a number of messages and coming back after going quiet. Free user ready to buy covers hitting the free daily limit.

Buyer covers bought access, bought again and refunded. Upsell timing covers an allowance running low and used up. Re-engage covers going quiet. Feedback covers a rating of 4 or more stars and a rating of 2 or fewer.

You set the thresholds, such as the number of messages. Write {bot} in a tag and it fills with the bot's slug. Leave a tag blank and that event isn't sent. As before, only customers who agreed to share their details are included.

**A tag set to start from**

For example, nine tags using the pattern name-{bot}:

> First conversation started: chatted-{bot}
> Signed up but never chatted: no-chat-{bot}
> Reached a number of messages: engaged-{bot}
> Hit the free daily limit: ready-to-buy-{bot}
> Bought access: buyer-{bot}
> Allowance running low: topup-{bot}
> Went quiet: re-engage-{bot}
> Rated 4 or more stars: happy-{bot}
> Rated 2 or fewer stars: unhappy-{bot}

Start with these and leave the rest blank. You can add more once your CRM shows you what it needs.

**Task for today**

In [Settings](https://lab.{{domain}}/settings) then CRM then Lifecycle tags, enter five of the tags above. Pick the five that match an action you'd really take, such as a follow-up email to anyone tagged ready-to-buy.

Speak soon,

Your Botter Support Team

## UP08 - Tag what they ask about (day 13, Pro upgrade)

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] Tag customers by what they ask about

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: last time we set up lifecycle tags. I said we'd tag customers by what they ask about and then see who has agreed to share their details.

New topic: today covers interest tags and the customer download. Both depend on the same consent as the CRM connector.

**Interest tags**

An interest tag is a keyword rule. When a customer's message contains one of your words or phrases, the tag is sent to your CRM, once per tag for each customer. Only the customer's messages are read and never the bot's replies. Matching is on whole words and ignores capital letters.

Open [Settings](https://lab.{{domain}}/settings) then CRM then Interest tags and press Add a rule. Fill in Tag and Keywords or phrases then choose Cover all my bots or pick under Bots this rule covers. Press Save. The [Create a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/create) page has an Interest tags section too (on a saved bot).

Plans allow a set number of rules. Rules over the limit are paused with the note Paused because your plan allows fewer rules.

**Three rules to start from**

For example:

> Tag: interest-pricing
> Keywords or phrases: price, pricing, cost, how much
>
> Tag: interest-booking
> Keywords or phrases: book, booking, appointment, availability
>
> Tag: interest-refund
> Keywords or phrases: refund, money back, cancel

Because matching is on whole words, "price" won't match "pricing". List both.

**The customer download**

Under Settings then CRM then Your customers, press Download customers (CSV). It lists the customers who agreed to share their details and haven't stopped, with their name, email address, the bot, where they agreed and the date. It never includes customers who didn't tick the box.

Customers can stop sharing at any time under Shared with bot owners in their own Settings. Honour every unsubscribe in your CRM.

**Task for today**

Add one interest rule then download the CSV. It may be short or empty while customers are still choosing to share.

Speak soon,

Your Botter Support Team

**What it looks like**

Open [A/B tests](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/ab-tests) in the menu on Master Botter and you pick a bot. The bot as it stands is the control. You set the Control's share (%) then add a variant with its own Variant name, Share (%), Instructions and Starters. The shares must total 100%.

While the test is Running, the same visitor always sees the same version. The results show Conversations, Messages per conversation, Returning customers, Paywall conversion and Revenue per conversation for each version.

Master Botter also adds a Conversion panel to [Analytics](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/analytics). It sets Paywall views and Listing views against Purchases so you can see where visitors stop. Price is never tested.

The [Pricing](https://{{domain}}/pricing/) page lists what Master Botter includes. There's no need to do anything about it.

**A test to picture**

For example:

> Control: your bot as it is now, 50%
> Variant name: Warmer greeting, 50%
> Instructions: the same as the control plus "Open every first reply with one short friendly sentence."

**Task for today**

Use what Pro already gives you to make a baseline. Open Analytics in the menu and note Conversations and Messages per conversation for one bot over the last month. Then change one thing on the bot, such as a conversation starter and look again next month.
