Tomorrow: your keys to the Lab.

Welcome aboard.

The Botterverse team

## S12 - Keep the lights on (day 12, all tiers, story)

**Subject:** [Botterverse] Dev's bot went quiet at two o'clock

**Preheader:** One switch stops it happening to you.

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}}

Dev sends his launch email at nine on Tuesday. His bot answers questions about the offer all morning. At two in the afternoon it goes quiet. Buyers type a question and nothing comes back. Dev finds out when someone replies to his email: "Is your bot broken?"

It was not broken. It was out of fuel. When credits cannot cover a message every bot you own stops answering. That includes the people who have already paid.

(Dev is made up. The problem is not.)

A launch is the worst time for a bot to run dry. Auto top-up is the switch that prevents it.

**What your credits do**

Every message a bot sends uses credits. You pay for your visitors' chats and for your own testing. Your plan adds credits each month and they expire at the next renewal. Credits you buy never expire. The credits pill at the top of every page shows your balance.

**Switch on Auto top-up**

1. Open Billing and credits in the menu.
2. Find Auto top-up.
3. Read the statement then press Agree and switch on.
4. Set Top up when my credits fall below. Pick a level that leaves room for a busy day.
5. Set Top-up amount. It is in dollars and you receive one credit per cent. For example $20 buys 2,000 credits.
6. Save.

You need an active plan and a card on file. You are emailed every time it charges. If your bank asks for approval the top-up is not retried. If your card is declined twice auto top-up switches itself off and tells you.

**Have a promo code?**

Find Promo code in Billing and credits. Enter it and press Redeem. The bonus credits show at once.

More tomorrow.

The Botterverse team

**Notes**
- Story: Dev. Under 120 words. Opens mid-scene. The bridge is one plain sentence. The how-to still works without the story.
- Vocabulary stage 3. The Botterverse arrived on day 9 so a light touch is enough here.
- Automation: A0 step 12. Skip if tag Power User is present.
- Facts from the guide: all bots stop answering everyone when credits cannot cover a message. Auto top-up labels and the statement and the card and the one-credit-per-cent rule and the decline rule. Promo codes add bonus credits only and show at once.
- The $20 example is marked "for example". No default values are printed.

## U1 - What a price tag looks like (day 17, Apprentice, story)

**Subject:** [Botterverse] Maya answers the same ten questions every Friday

**Preheader:** She stopped. Here is what her bot's page looks like.

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}}

Every Friday Maya answers the same ten questions from her coaching clients. Same questions. Different people. She types the answers out again anyway.

One Friday she pastes them into a bot instead. Her Fridays are hers again. Then she puts a price on it.

(Maya is made up. The problem is not.)

Here is what her bot's page looks like to a buyer.

> **Friday Answers**
> Rating: appears once buyers have rated it
>
> **Things you can ask it**
> How do I handle a client who keeps cancelling?
> What goes in my welcome pack?
> Which package should a new client start with?
>
> **$19 one-time** for 100 messages (example)
> Top up 50 messages for $9.00 (example)
> Money-back guarantee: 7 days
>
> Pay

That page is the paywall. It is part of Novice Botter. Each message a buyer sends is paid from the bot owner's credits so a message cap keeps the price honest.

**What you can do today on Apprentice**

Write down the ten questions your own audience asks most. Answer each one in plain words. Save them in one document and upload it as a knowledge file. You built your first bot a few days ago. This makes it far more useful and it is the same material a paywall page would sell.

**When you are ready for a price**

On Novice Botter the steps are short:
1. Open the bot and find Paywall and pricing.
2. Switch on Sell access to this bot.
3. Enter the One-time price.
4. Save.

To move up open Billing and credits then Your plan then Change plan.

Paste this into the bot's description if you want a starting point: "A coaching assistant trained on the ten questions clients ask most. Ask it anything from handling cancellations to choosing a first package."

The Botterverse team

**Notes**
- Story: Maya. Opens mid-scene. The bridge is "Here is what her bot's page looks like to a buyer."
- Show not tell: the mock paywall page uses the guide's fields. No rating is invented. The price and top-up are labelled as examples. The top-up wording is the guide's own example.
- One plan sentence. No countdown. No pressure.
- Automation: T-App day 17. Only Apprentice.
- Vocabulary stage 4 (full mix). It is kept light here.
