## UE02 - Top conversation starters (day 1, Expert Botter)

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] What are your visitors really asking?

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Yesterday's tour introduced the deeper analytics. Your conversation starters hold a clue to what visitors want, so we begin there.

**Where starters show up**

Visitors see your starters when they open the bot. A paid bot also shows them on the paywall under Things you can ask it.

Top conversation starters on [Analytics](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/analytics) counts your starters. Choose a Bot and a Date range and read the list from the top.

**Rewriting your starters**

Starters at the top tell you what visitors reach for first. Starters at the bottom may be wasting space.

Paste this into any AI chat tool you use and fill in the blanks:

> Here are my bot's conversation starters, from most used to least used: [PASTE THE LIST]. The bot does this: [ONE SENTENCE]. Write six new starters in the words a first-time visitor would use. Keep each one under twelve words. Make the first two answer the same kind of question as my top two.

**Task for today**

Open the bot from [Bot list](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots) and select Conversation starters. Use Remove on the weakest one or two and Add a starter with your favourite new ones. Press Save.

Top conversation starters counts conversations that opened with one of your current starters, whether the visitor pressed it or typed the same words. Conversation insights, coming up in a few days, looks at the other questions they typed.

Enjoy the rewrite,

Your Botter Support Team

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Last time you rewrote your starters from what visitors asked. You know what they ask and today you find out when.

**Busiest hours**

Busiest hours on [Analytics](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/analytics) shows when visitors chat with a bot. Choose the Bot and a Date range and look for the stretch that repeats across days. The hours follow the timezone set on your account.

Share your link just before that stretch because that's when people are most ready to try it.

**Daily credit limit**

On a busy day a free bot can use many credits. In Sharing and embed on the [Create a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/create) page, Daily credit limit caps that. Leave it empty to use the platform default or enter a whole number of credits.

At the limit free visitors are told the bot has reached its limit for the day. Paid buyers and you carry on. The count resets at midnight UTC and you're emailed once that day.

**A simple posting plan**

1. Open Busiest hours and note the top three times.
2. Choose one of those and schedule a post or email with your Share link for just before it.
3. Check Usage this month on the [Dashboard](https://lab.{{domain}}/) to see what a normal day costs.
4. Set a Daily credit limit a little above that and press Save.

**Task for today**

Follow the four steps above for your busiest bot. Next week compare Conversations on Analytics with the week before.

Speak soon,

Your Botter Support Team

**How to use a suggestion**

Choose the suggestion that matches your most common unanswered question. Press Add to knowledge if it's a fact and Add to instructions if it's a rule about how the bot should behave.

## UE07 - Credits at a bigger scale and Auto top-up (day 11, Expert Botter)

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] Keeping your credits from running out

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Last time you planned a set of bots. More bots mean more messages, so today you make sure your credits don't run out.

**Where credits come from**

Included credits are added with your plan and reset at each renewal or plan change. Credits you buy never expire. Select [Billing and credits](https://lab.{{domain}}/billing) to see them. The sections run in this order: Credits and usage, Auto top-up, Promo code, Buy credits and Your plan.

The usage graph shows which days used the most. The history labels tell you whether credits came from Plan credits or Credits bought.

If your credits can't cover a message every one of your bots stops answering everyone, buyers included. You're emailed when that happens.

**Setting up Auto top-up**

1. Open Auto top-up and read the statement.
2. Press Agree and switch on.
3. Enter Top up when my credits fall below as a whole number of credits.
4. Enter the Top-up amount in dollars. You receive one credit per cent and there's no pack bonus.

For example, below 500 credits with a top-up of 10 dollars. Choose figures that suit your own usage.

You'll need an active plan and a card on file. You're emailed each time it charges. A daily cap pauses it until the next day. If your card is declined twice it switches off with an email.

**Task for today**

Switch on Auto top-up with your own figures. Then press the credits pill at the top and check the usage graph.

Stay topped up,

Your Botter Support Team

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] Which setting should you change first?

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Last time you switched on Auto top-up. Your numbers can also tune your paywall, so today you decide which setting to change first.

**Three settings the data can guide**

Message cap and top-up. If credits per message on [Analytics](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/analytics) is high the cap may be too generous. If buyers reach it and don't top up the top-up size may be too large.

Starters. Top conversation starters shows which ones open the most conversations. They show on the paywall under Things you can ask it.

Description. If visitors reach the paywall and don't buy the description may not say what the bot does. Compare conversion with Category benchmarks.

**A price change affects new buyers only**

Existing buyers keep what they paid. Change one setting at a time and give it a week before you judge.

**A short tuning checklist**

1. Open Analytics and choose the bot and the last month.
2. Write down conversations, credits per message and conversion.
3. Change one thing: the cap, the starters or the description.
4. Press Save.
5. Check the same three figures a week later.

**Task for today**

Choose the one setting from the list and change it. Write the date and the old value so you can compare.

Good luck with it,

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Last time you planned a style for your bots. What if someone wanted to buy the bot itself? Today is a look at selling on the Bot Market.

**What selling involves**

Pro Botter and above can list a Public bot on the Bot Market. There are two sale types. Transfer moves ownership and sells once. Multi-sell gives each buyer their own copy under your licence and you keep the original.

To list a bot select [My listings](https://lab.{{domain}}/listings) then List a bot for sale. The form includes Description and Tags with a Suggest a description and tags button that uses no credits. It also has Sale type and licence, Price (USD) and Affiliate share (%).

You choose one of four licences: Personal use, Resell rights, Master resell rights or PLR. See the [Pricing](https://{{domain}}/pricing/) page for the plan.

**A sample description**

> A plain-English guide to [TOPIC] for [WHO IT IS FOR]. Built from [WHAT IT KNOWS]. Ask it [THING ONE] or [THING TWO].

**Task for today**

You can browse the Bot Market now. Select [Browse](https://lab.{{domain}}/market) then switch on 4 stars and up. Open three listings and note what each description says and which licence it uses. That's your research for the day you list one.
