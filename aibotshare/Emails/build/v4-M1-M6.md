## M2 - Earning 2: message cap, top-up and money-back guarantee (day 10, Novice and above)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] Cap it and add a promise

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday you put a price on your bot and ended with the question of what stops a paywall from costing you.

New topic: today you cap the messages and add a promise.

Each message a buyer sends is paid for from your boosts. A message cap protects you from an open-ended bill and a guarantee protects the buyer.

**Message cap and top-up**

Open the bot from [Bot list](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots) and go to Paywall and pricing. Three fields work as a set:

1. Message cap is the number of messages one purchase includes. Empty means no cap.
2. Top-up size in messages is how many messages a buyer can add.
3. Top-up price is what that extra costs.

A top-up needs both fields and a cap. When a buyer reaches the cap, they see Top up.

For example, a Message cap of 200 with a top-up of 50 messages. These are illustrations only and the app applies minimum prices.

**Money-back guarantee**

Choose No guarantee or 7 days or 14 days. Only periods within the platform refund period are offered and the wording on the paywall is fixed.

If a buyer claims a refund under a guarantee, the full price comes out of your earnings. So don't promise a longer period elsewhere, because you may be unable to refund it.

**Task for today**

Set a Message cap with a top-up that suits your bot and choose a guarantee. Press Save and then check the paywall in a private window.

Keep it up,

Your Botter Support Team

P.S. Next up: now your bot can earn but who tells people? Tomorrow, you meet the affiliate programme.

**Notes**
- Facts: Message cap empty means no cap. Top-up size in messages and Top-up price. A top-up needs both fields and a cap. At the cap the buyer sees Top up. Guarantee choices No guarantee and 7 days and 14 days within the platform refund period. A guarantee refund takes the full price from earnings (5.2 to 5.4 and 7.3).
- "14 days" appears only as a guarantee option the guide lists and not as a platform refund promise.
- Example cap and top-up size are messages and not prices. No dollar figure is used.
- Automation: Welcome Earning track step 2 (day 10). Condition: Novice or Expert or Pro or Master.

## M6 - Earning 6: capture leads and send them home (day 14, Pro and above)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] Send your leads home

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday you prepared a listing. A Public bot brings visitors and today the ones who agree to share their details can reach your own CRM.

New topic: the CRM connector is included from Pro Botter. It sends a customer's details and actions to your own CRM by webhook once the customer agrees.

**Know who is chatting**

Open the bot from [Bot list](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots). In Sharing and embed, switch on Require visitors to sign up so visitors sign up or sign in before they chat. A new bot has it on already. Save the bot first, because Sharing and embed only works once it's saved.

In the same section, the Daily boost limit caps what the bot can spend in a day. Leave it empty to use the platform default or enter whole boosts. It resets at midnight UTC and we email you once that day.

**How consent works**

Nothing is sent unless the customer agrees. While the connection is on, an unticked box asks the customer to share their name and email address with you. It's never required.

**Connecting your CRM**

1. Open [Settings](https://lab.{{domain}}/settings) and select CRM.
2. In Webhook address, enter an address that starts with https. In Your privacy notice, enter an address too, because it's required.
3. Press Save. Your signing secret then appears once, so copy it somewhere safe. Your developer can use it to check each event is genuine.
4. Press Send test event and check it arrives in your CRM.
5. Press Switch on.

Each event carries the event name, its tag and the customer's name and email address. It also holds the bot's name and slug and the time. You choose the tag for each event in Settings under CRM and then Lifecycle tags. A blank tag means that event isn't sent.

Once connected, you're responsible for the data under data protection law and you need to honour every unsubscribe. If the connection keeps failing, it switches itself off and we email you.

**Task for today**

Switch on Require visitors to sign up and set a Daily boost limit on one Public bot. Then enter your Webhook address and Your privacy notice and press Save. Press Send test event before you Switch on.

Good luck,

Your Botter Support Team

P.S. Next up: your bot can call your own tools while it chats. Tomorrow you plug it into them.

**Notes**
- Facts: CRM connector is Pro Botter and above. Settings then CRM. Field and button labels and the one-time signing secret and event contents and blank tag rule and responsibility for data and automatic switch-off from 9.2 and 9.3 and 9.5. Require visitors to sign up and Daily boost limit sit in Sharing and embed and need the bot saved (3.1 and 3.3 and 4.4).
- Consent condition is stated. The email never says all your customers.
- Left out on purpose: header names and signing algorithm (for the member's developer) and the A/B variant field.
- Automation: Welcome Earning track step 6 (day 14). Condition: tier tag Pro or Master sends this version. Apprentice and Novice and Expert receive the M6 preview.
