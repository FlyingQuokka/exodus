# Hook shortlist 1

Four Hook Forge runs on 20 October 2026, about 80 hooks in total, one pool per angle. The raw pools are in the four folders beside this file. This is my vetted shortlist. Every pick is rewritten to meet the copy rules and checked against the brief.

## How I vetted

A hook is rejected if it does any of these:
- speaks as a founder or invents a story ("I watched a marketer", "I had 400 people")
- invents a number, a result or an anecdote
- makes an absolute claim nobody can prove ("no marketplace has ever")
- implies income or leans on money results
- claims a feature the brief does not support
- compares people to slavery or other offensive images

## The big premise to check before anything is published

Several of the strongest ownership hooks say you "never had your buyers' emails". That is true of GPT users in general. It may be false for imers who sold access through their own checkout, because their payment processor holds the emails. What a GPT seller genuinely lost is sight of who used the bot and any way to message those users through the platform. I rewrote the picks around that safer truth. Your Skool material will show which version lands.

Settled on 21 October: the export includes no buyer names and no emails, to keep data protection responsibility with AI Bot Share. Still unconfirmed: what a seller can see about members who sign up on their bot. No copy says "customer list" or "email list".

## What I rejected and why

- **Every first-person confession** (about 14 hooks). They break founder anonymity and invent figures such as "3,200 people", "4,000 AI buyers" and "$4,000 on traffic".
- **"No marketplace has ever let you sell a bot with PLR rights."** An absolute claim nobody can prove.
- **The 1850 sharecropper comparison.** Offensive and a reputational risk.
- **"Thousands of builders woke up with products they could no longer sell."** Overclaims. The GPT Store still exists and creation was closed to personal accounts.
- **"You can't even set your own price."** Inaccurate. Imers set prices on their own offers.
- **"Analytics on every bot."** Analytics start at the Novice plan.
- **"One platform right now that lets you..."** A superlative claim.
- **Money-implying lines** such as "generating exactly zero dollars" and "quiet millionaires".
