## The product (one paragraph for context)

Botterverse is a platform where people build, share and sell AI chatbots. A member builds a bot in the Botters Lab by writing instructions and adding files. They pick an AI model then share the bot by link or embed it on their own site. They can charge for access and list the bot on Bot Market. From 16 August 2026 OpenAI stopped personal ChatGPT accounts from creating or publishing new custom GPTs, so people who built and sold custom GPTs need a new home. Members who bring in new members receive ongoing commission through the affiliate programme.

## Plans (monthly list prices)

Apprentice $5 (1 bot). Novice $47 (20 bots and selling access). Expert $97 (50 bots). Pro $197 (unlimited bots and Bot Market selling). Master $297. Annual billing is ten times the monthly price. Never describe it as months for nothing. Never mention a sixth tier.

## Audience

1. People who sign up on the Founders page during the deal. They already know the offer. The emails keep them moving to the checkout before the deadline.
2. Affiliates. They promote Botterverse to their own lists and audiences. Commission is 50% of a member's first payment and 20% of every later payment, on the price paid after tax. The programme starts on Novice. Commission is on the price paid, so at 50% off the commission is on half the list price.

## Worked arithmetic for affiliates (use it, it is what makes the copy land)

Black Friday (50% off) on monthly billing, commission on the first payment:
- Novice $47: buyer pays $23.50, affiliate receives $11.75, then $4.70 on each later payment
- Expert $97: buyer pays $48.50, affiliate receives $24.25
- Pro $197: buyer pays $98.50, affiliate receives $49.25
- Master $297: buyer pays $148.50, affiliate receives $74.25

An affiliate on Novice at 50% off pays $23.50 per month. One Pro referral pays $49.25, which covers two months. One Master referral pays $74.25, which covers three months. Two Pro referrals cover four months. The Founder discount applies to monthly and annual billing. Annual is ten times monthly: a Pro annual buyer at 50% off pays $985 and the affiliate receives $492.50 on that first payment, which covers two years of Novice annual at $235 a year. At 40% off a Pro annual buyer pays $1,182 and the affiliate receives $591, which covers two years of Novice annual at $282 a year.

Festive (40% off): Pro monthly buyer pays $118.20 and the affiliate receives $59.10. Master monthly buyer pays $178.20 and the affiliate receives $89.10. Novice at 40% off is $28.20 per month, so one Pro referral covers two months with a little left over.

Tax is excluded from the base. State figures as arithmetic. Do not promise anyone will make sales.

## Voice

British English. No comma before "and" or "or". Hyphens only and never an em or en dash. Never the word "get" in any form. No exclamation marks except sparingly. Contractions are fine. Plain words and no flowery language. Warm and direct. Emotion comes from the stakes: the custom GPT loss, ownership, a fixed price that never goes up for a Founder, a clock that is real, and for affiliates the arithmetic of a membership that pays for itself.

## Hard rules

- No testimonials and no invented people or stories. No competitor names (say "custom GPTs are being switched off").
- Never use: free, guaranteed, instant, unlimited (except the approved Pro wording "unlimited bots"), "make money", "earn". For affiliates say "commission".
- No Founder badge. No mention of the EU. No mention of a sixth tier. No numbers of members or sales.
- Botters Lab is the members area. Never "Bot Cave". Brand is Botterverse. The old name never appears.
- Email subjects use the prefix `[Botterverse]`. Links in FluentCRM emails use `https://{{domain}}/founders/?cpn=founder50` (or `founder40`). Never type a domain.
- Affiliate swipe emails use `{affiliate_link}` as the only link (the app builds the full link). They start straight in. No disclaimer lines and no "affiliate link" disclosure line (the owner decided).
- Say the deadline as "at the close of 1st December EST". On the final day "tonight at midnight Eastern" is fine. Never write a clock time such as 23:59:59. In FluentCRM emails use `{{cpn_dateend_founder50}}`.
