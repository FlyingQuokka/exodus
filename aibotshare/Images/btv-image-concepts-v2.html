Botterverse - Image concepts v2

Ten new illustrations built around the Botterverse idea: one shared world of islands and bridges and workshops where bots of every shape live. Each is drawn as pure vector shapes so it stays sharp and light. Each has a job on the home page. None shows income or results or a named competitor or a real person. Use the button to see the Nightshift version.

1Welcome to the Botterverse

A friendly bot on its home island with bridges to neighbours under a ringed planet. It shows a shared world rather than a lone tool.

Use: Hero (section 1)

2The workshop

A bot builds a smaller bot under a string of lamps. Gears turn above and a blueprint hangs on the wall.

Use: Build card (section 2) and step 3 of How it works (section 6)

3Send it across

One bot and one link. Glowing trails carry the link to three islands where other bots wave back.

Use: Share card (section 2) and Your link or your site (section 4)

4A bot on your page

One small snippet floats from a bot island onto a web page. A chat bubble opens and the visitor has an answer.

Use: Your link or your site (section 4) and Share card (section 2)

5Crossing the bridge

A bot leaves a switched-off island with its files in a box. It crosses a bridge to a bright house of its own.

Use: Switch-off block (section 3)

6The market square

A floating square with bunting and two stalls run by bots. A visitor bot browses between them.

Use: Bot Market card (section 4) and the Pro plan row (section 8)

7Pick a model

Four different planets orbit one island. The bot points a glowing beam at the one it wants. Nothing is chosen for it.

Use: Your model card (section 4) and the model choice in Build (section 2)

8Checked at the gate

A bot with a magnifier looks over each visitor at a stone gate. Bots that pass wear a tick. A big shield glows overhead.

Use: Checked bots (section 7)

9Follow the trail

A bot waves a flag and a trail of lights leads new bots from island to island. A wrapped gift waits at the end.

Use: Earn by referring (section 9)

10The credit tank

A tall glass tank of crystals shows the balance. Small bots carry more in and a gauge shows how full it is.

Use: Who pays when people chat (section 10) and Plans credits (section 8)
