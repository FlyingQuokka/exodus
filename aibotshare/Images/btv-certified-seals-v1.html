Certified seals, version 1

Six seal styles for the Certified by Botterverse mark. It appears on Bot Market listings and in the footer of embedded chats. Each is a traditional seal shape with the bot in the middle and the tick offset to the lower right. The drawing is cartoony but the layout is formal so the mark reads as official. The first four carry the words. The last two drop the words so they stay clear at small sizes. Each seal shows at full size on light and on Nightshift and then at 64 and 32 and 20 pixels.
