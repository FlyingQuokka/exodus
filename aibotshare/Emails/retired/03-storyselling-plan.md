# Storyselling plan

Draft 1 for review. This plan says which emails open with a story and how each story works. The stories themselves are written with the emails.

## 1. The rule that shapes everything

No testimonials exist and none may be invented. A story about a real-sounding customer would be a fabricated testimonial. So every story is openly made up.

- The characters are invented Botters. The first story that uses each one carries a light label such as "Maya is made up. The problem is not."
- No real people. No named brands. No income figures. No results claims.
- A story shows a problem or a way of working. It never reports a result as if it happened.

## 2. How a story email is built

1. **The scene.** Start in the middle of something. No "Imagine this" and no "Picture this".
2. **The snag.** The exact problem the feature solves. Keep it small and real.
3. **The bridge.** One plain sentence that turns to the feature. Never "And that is exactly why".
4. **The how-to.** The normal body of the email. It must still work if the story is deleted.

Limits:
- 120 words at most for the story.
- One story per email.
- At least one email without a story between two story emails in any member's sequence.
- The story must be about the same thing as the email. If the link is forced the story is cut.

Bridge patterns to vary:
- A turn: "The fix was one setting."
- A hand-over: "Here is how she set it up. Yours takes the same steps."
- A question: "What would she have needed? A cap."
