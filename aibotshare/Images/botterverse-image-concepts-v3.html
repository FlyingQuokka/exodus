Botterverse - Image concepts v3

Ten new illustrations with no sky and no islands and no ground. Each one draws a single line of the home page copy as a clear object or scene. The Botterverse bot keeps its two purple feet in every picture. Nothing shows income or a named competitor. Use the button to see Nightshift.

1The remote

Illustrates: “Who needs custom GPTs when you can build bots you control?” - Section 1 Hero.

The bot holds a remote with dials and sliders and one big button while its other hand moves a slider. Control is the whole picture.

2The bot reads its brief

Illustrates: “Write what the bot should do in plain language.” - Section 2 Build.

The bot holds up a sheet headed What the bot should do and reads the plain lines on it. What you write is what it follows.

3Feeding time

Illustrates: “Upload PDF or Word or text or Markdown or CSV files.” - Section 4 Your knowledge.

Five file tiles stream towards a hungry bot with its mouth wide open. The files are what it knows.

4Paste and appear

Illustrates: “Send a link or paste one snippet into your site.” - Section 2 Share.

A web page with a link in its address bar and one snippet pasted below. A chat bubble from the bot appears on the page.

5Ticket and shield

Illustrates: “Charge a one-time price for access. Set a message cap with a top-up. Add a money-back guarantee if you want one.” - Section 2 Sell.

A ticket with a blank price tag and a message meter with a plus button and a shield with a tick. No figure is shown.

6Unplugged and reborn

Illustrates: “Custom GPTs are being switched off” and “Bring your GPT across.” - Section 3 the switch-off block.

On the left a plug is pulled out and the bot goes dim and grey. On the right a pasted copy comes out of the Import a bot slot in full colour.

7The model dial

Illustrates: “Pick it for each bot. Nothing is chosen for you.” - Section 3 and Section 4 Choose the model.

The bot turns a big rotary dial with four model names around it. The choice sits in the bot’s own hand.

8Faces and names

Illustrates: “Ask visitors to sign up and you see who they are.” - Section 3 Know who is chatting.

One chat shown two ways. Anonymous grey silhouettes on the left and named visitors with initials on the right after a sign-up form.

9The checked card

Illustrates: “Every bot is checked and says what it does” - Section 7 Checked bots.

A certificate with the bot and a declarations checklist while a magnifying glass looks closely. The card states that it does not vouch for the answers.

10The guarded tap

Illustrates: “Set a daily credit limit on a bot and a message cap on a paid bot so a busy day cannot use up your balance.” - Section 10 Questions (who pays when people chat).

A tall tank of credits with a marked limit line while the bot holds the tap shut above it. The balance stays safe on a busy day.
