## T1 - A quick tour (day 1, all tiers)

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: five minutes ago we covered what your plan includes and how to create your Botter's Lab account. If you haven't created it yet, the link in your purchase email is the place to start.

New topic: here's a short tour of what you'll see when you sign in, so nothing is a surprise.

**Signing in**

The [Sign in](https://lab.{{domain}}/login) page asks for your username and your password. It never asks for your email address. If you forget your username, press Forgotten username? and we'll email it to you.

On a computer or phone only you use, tick Keep me signed in. That saves you typing your details every time.

**The Dashboard**

The [Dashboard](https://lab.{{domain}}/) is the first page you see after you sign in. Credit balance is what you can spend on messages right now and Usage this month shows how many credits you've used.

Active bots shows how many bots you have against your plan's limit, for example 3 of 5. Revenue over time will show your share of what your bots earn.

**The menu**

The menu down the left is how you move around. Bots holds [Bot list](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots), [Import a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/import), Analytics, A/B tests and Bot styles. Bot Market holds Browse, Bots I bought, My listings and My profile.

Earnings holds Sales and Payouts. Account holds [Billing and credits](https://lab.{{domain}}/billing). Settings, Support, Help, Feedback and Affiliates sit at the foot.

Your plan name shows under your name at the top right. A page your plan doesn't include has a short note saying what it does and which plans have it, so you can look without anything breaking.

**Task for today**

[Sign in](https://lab.{{domain}}/login) with your username and tick Keep me signed in. Then find your Credit balance on the Dashboard and open Bot list to see where your bots will live.

For a quick win, press the theme button at the top to switch between Pleasant Pink and Nightshift.

See you shortly,

Your Botter Support Team

## T2 - Bring your existing bot in (day 1, all tiers)

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] Bring your existing bot in

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: earlier today, you had a tour of signing in, the Dashboard and the menu. Your account is confirmed and your plan is in place.

New topic: if you already have a bot somewhere else you don't need to start again. [Import a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/import) brings it into the Botter's Lab by pasting and uses no credits.

**What you can bring**

A custom GPT is a good example. You paste in its instructions and upload its files. It doesn't connect to any other account and nothing is saved until the last step.

1. Open Import a bot from the menu.
2. In Instructions, paste your instructions and press Continue.
3. In Files, press Browse and choose the files the bot should use.
4. In Integrations, press Add integration if the bot calls another service. This step is optional and isn't part of Apprentice Botter.
5. In Review, check the suggested Name, Description, Category and Starters. Not carried across lists anything that couldn't move.
6. Press Create bot.

The bot opens in the builder as Personal, so only you can use it. It counts towards your plan's bot limit like any other bot.

**Task for today**

Import one bot. Read Not carried across before you change anything and then try the bot in Preview. Preview uses your credits.

If you have no bot to bring in, you don't need to do anything today because tomorrow's email builds one with you.

Welcome home,

Your Botter Support Team

P.S. Next up: how to build a bot from scratch in a single sitting.
