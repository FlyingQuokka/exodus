# Home headline options, draft 1

Two angles with 20 headlines each. Rules applied: no competitor named, no date for the GPT switch-off, no promised speed or income, no claim that members own their customers.

## Proposed hero (a mix of both angles)

**Headline:** Who needs custom GPTs when you can build bots you control?

**Sub-headline:** Build a bot. Share it. Sell access to it. You choose the model and set the price.

**Button:** Take Me To The Plans

The third idea ("Turn what you know into a bot people pay to use") moves into the page. It becomes the heading of the examples section and the closing call.
