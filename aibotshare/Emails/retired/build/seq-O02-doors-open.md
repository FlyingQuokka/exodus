## O02-02 - From plan to live bot (day 2, Leads)

**Subject:** [Botterverse] From plan to a live bot

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Last time I promised the path from plan to a live bot. Here it is in six steps.

**The path**

1. Choose a plan on the Pricing page. Buy with the email address you'll register with.
2. Create your Botter's Lab account from the second link in your purchase email.
3. Confirm your email and sign in with your username.
4. Build the bot. Name it, write what it should do in plain language, add your files and choose the model.
5. Preview it. You chat as a visitor would and nobody else sees it.
6. Press Make Public. Every bot is checked first.

Every new bot starts as Personal and goes live only when you make it Public. Previewing uses credits from your balance, as does every visitor message.

**Next step**

Pick one topic you could brief a new assistant on. That's the whole starting point for a bot.

## O02-03 - Bring a GPT across (day 4, Leads)

**Subject:** [Botterverse] Bring your custom GPT across

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

In the last email we followed the path from plan to a live bot. If you've built a custom GPT you can skip most of the building.

**Import a bot**

Open [Import a bot](https://members.{{domain}}/bots/import) in the Botter's Lab menu. Paste in the GPT's instructions and add its files. It doesn't connect to your OpenAI account so you bring the content across yourself.

The last step is Review. It suggests a name, description, category and conversation starters. A list called Not carried across shows anything that couldn't move. Press Create bot to open it as a Personal bot you can edit and preview.

Importing uses no credits. If your GPT used actions you rebuild them as integrations and those start on the Novice Botter plan.

**Next step**

Collect your GPT's instructions and files in one folder so the import takes a few minutes once you're in.

Speak soon,

Your Botter Team

## O02-04 - Three ways to reach people (day 6, Leads)

**Subject:** [Botterverse] Three ways to put a bot in front of people

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Last time we covered Import a bot. Once a bot is built the next question is how people find it.

**Share a link**

Every Public bot has its own page with your username in the address. Copy the Share link and send it anywhere.

**Put it on your site**

Copy the Embed snippet then paste it into your site's custom code or HTML box. By default a new bot asks visitors to sign up so the embed shows a button that opens the bot's page. Turn that setting off and the chat runs in the page for free bots. Visitors are always told they're talking to an AI.

**List it on Bot Market**

Bot Market is where Botters trade bots with each other. Every plan can buy there and selling starts on the Pro Botter plan.

No plan promises sales. Reaching people is up to you and these are the tools.

**Next step**

Decide which of the three fits your audience best. If you already have a website the embed is usually the quickest.

Speak soon,

Your Botter Team

## O02-05 - Cost and control (day 8, Leads)

**Subject:** [Botterverse] What it costs and what stays yours

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

In the last email we looked at the three ways to reach people. Today is cost and control.

**What it costs**

There's no free plan or trial. You pay for a plan and your plan includes credits each month. Credits pay for the messages a bot sends and you can buy more at any time. The [Pricing](https://{{domain}}/pricing/) page lists the plans and credit packs.

A Daily credit limit on a bot and a Message cap on a paid bot stop a busy day from using up your balance.

**What stays yours**

You can cancel from [Billing and credits](https://members.{{domain}}/billing) when you like and your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. Request an export from [Settings](https://members.{{domain}}/settings) and your bots, knowledge and conversations go with you.

**Next step**

Read the [FAQ](https://{{domain}}/faq/) and note any question it doesn't answer. Send it through [Support](https://{{domain}}/support/) and we'll reply.
