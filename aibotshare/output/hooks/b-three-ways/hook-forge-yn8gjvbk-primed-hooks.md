Again.

The published-fee contrast in the second beat gives them the immediate escape route: here's the version where you actually know what you earn.

⚡ **HOOK 1** — "Your $17 PLR ebook has three revenue paths." This hook works because it uses the reader's own expertise as the weapon.

DANGEROUS because it attacks the PRESENT, not the past. Every other hook in the set says "you made a mistake before." This one says "you're making it RIGHT NOW." And the closing line — "the only thing that changed is the landlord's name" — is the kind of sentence that makes someone put their phone down and stare at the ceiling. It reframes their entire current rebuild as a repetition of the exact failure they thought they escaped.
