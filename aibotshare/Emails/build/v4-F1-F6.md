## F1 - Integrations (day 15, Novice and above)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] Plug your bot into your tools

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: in the last email you saw how to capture leads and send them home. Next up was plugging your bot into your tools, which is today.

New topic: an integration lets your bot call one of your own services in the middle of a chat. That could be a booking system, an order lookup or a price list.

**Adding an integration**

Say a visitor asks when you're free. The bot checks your booking system and answers with real times instead of a guess.

Save the bot first because integrations only work on a saved bot. Open the bot from [Bot list](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots) then go to Integrations on the [Create a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/create) page and press Add an integration.

1. Fill in Name, Method, What it does and Endpoint address. The address must start with https.
2. Press Add a parameter for each detail the bot should send, such as a date. Choose the Type and Sent in values your service expects.
3. Choose your Authentication: None, Bearer token, API key in a header or Basic (user name and password).
4. Check Switched on and press Save integration.

For example:

> Name: Check free times
> What it does: Looks up free appointment times for the date the visitor asks about. Use it whenever someone asks when we're available.
> Endpoint address: https://[YOUR SERVICE ADDRESS]
> Parameter name: date

Your credentials are stored encrypted and never shown again. An integration takes effect as soon as you save it.

If it sends data to anyone other than the model provider, that needs declaring the next time you confirm your bot's declarations.

**Task for today**

Add one integration to a bot that needs your service then ask it a question in Preview. Preview uses your boosts.

Happy connecting,

Your Botter Support Team

P.S. Next up: reading the numbers. Tomorrow, you see how your bot is doing.

**Notes**
- Facts: Integrations are Novice Botter and above. Save the bot first. Field and button labels, the four Authentication options and credential storage from 9.1. Declaration 7 and re-confirmation after an integration change from 4.2. Preview uses the owner's boosts (3.9).
- The Method options are not listed in the digest so none are named.

## F4 - Daily limit and sign-up requirement (day 19, all tiers)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] Two settings for the door

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday we looked at the busy hours and Conversation insights. Next up was setting the doors, which is today.

New topic: two settings decide who comes in and how much a bot can spend. One makes visitors sign up and the other caps the boosts a bot uses in a day.

**Require visitors to sign up**

When it's on, visitors sign up or sign in before they chat. You know who is chatting and a paid bot always needs an account anyway.

When it's off, anyone can chat at once. It's on for a new bot. While it's on, an embedded bot shows a Chat with button and not the chat itself.

**Daily boost limit**

You pay for every visitor message so this protects your boosts. Leave it empty to use the platform default or enter a number of whole boosts.

When a bot reaches the limit, free visitors are told it has reached its limit for the day. Paid buyers and you carry on. The limit resets at midnight UTC and we email you once that day.

Both sit in the Sharing and embed section of the [Create a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/create) page. Open the bot from [Bot list](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots) and set them like this then press Save:

> Require visitors to sign up: on
> Daily boost limit: [A WHOLE NUMBER OF BOOSTS YOU'RE HAPPY TO SPEND ON THIS BOT IN ONE DAY]

**Task for today**

Open a bot that collects leads. Set both fields and press Save.

Until tomorrow,

Your Botter Support Team

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday you set the Daily boost limit and Require visitors to sign up. Next up was dressing your bot, which is today.

New topic: bot styles are included from Pro Botter. A style changes how a bot looks to visitors and not how Botters Lab looks to you.

**What a style changes**

A style applies to the chat page, the paywall, the embed and the sign-up pages for a bot. Open [Bot styles](https://lab.{{domain}}/bot-styles) from the menu and press New style. A Live preview shows your changes as you go and a warning appears if the contrast is poor.

A style has four groups of settings:

- Branding: your logo (jpg or png or webp) and a Display name
- Colours: Background, Bot message bubble, Customer message bubble, Text and Accent
- Text and corners: Font, Chat text size (Small, Medium or Large) and Corners (Square, Soft or Round)
- What shows: Show the header, Show the bot's image and Show the powered-by tag

The powered-by tag carries your affiliate link. It earns only once you've joined the affiliate programme.

Apply a style with the Style section of the [Create a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/create) page or with Apply to another bot. Edit changes every bot that uses the style and Duplicate as a new style makes a copy.

**Task for today**

## F6 - Interest tags and the customer download (day 21, Pro and above)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] Tag them by interest

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday you gave a bot its own style. Next up was tagging what they ask about, which is today.

New topic: interest tags are keyword rules. When a customer's message contains your keyword, the bot sends the tag to your CRM.

**How the rules work**

A rule matches whole words and ignores capitals. It reads customer messages only and never the bot's replies. Each tag is sent once per customer. Tags are sent only for customers who agreed to share their details.

Open [Settings](https://lab.{{domain}}/settings) and select CRM then Interest tags. Press Add a rule and fill in Tag and Keywords or phrases. Choose Cover all my bots or pick the bots under Bots this rule covers. Then press Save.

Because matching uses whole words, add each form you need, such as book and booking. For example:

> Tag: interest-pricing
> Keywords or phrases: price, cost, how much
>
> Tag: interest-booking
> Keywords or phrases: book, booking, appointment

**Your customers**

Under Settings then CRM, you'll also find Your customers. Download customers (CSV) lists the customers who agreed to share their details and haven't stopped. Each row holds the name, email address, bot, where they agreed and the date.

Customers can stop sharing at any time under Shared with bot owners in their own Settings.

**Task for today**

Add the first example rule for a bot that collects leads and press Save. Download customers (CSV) once some customers have agreed.
