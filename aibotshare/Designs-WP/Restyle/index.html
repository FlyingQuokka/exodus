Botterverse restyle samples

Eight versions of the home page and pricing. They use the real copy and the real logo files. Fonts load from Google Fonts so you need to be online.

Pink and more professional (A to E)

A. Quiet pink

Smallest step away from today. Nunito Sans headings, deep purple buttons with white text, 12px corners, light shadows, a dark top banner and a product screenshot in the hero.

B. Studio pink

Manrope headings with tight spacing, 8px corners and hairline borders with no shadows. A flat tidy look that still has pink washes and a small mascot.

C. Editorial pink

A warm serif for headings (Fraunces) over Nunito Sans body text. Friendly and a little literary, with a pink wash hero and calm outlined cards.

D. Gentle pink

Keeps the pill buttons and soft shadows but changes the font to Plus Jakarta Sans and the buttons to purple. The softest of the five, with a larger mascot.

E. Baloo refined

Keeps the current Baloo 2 headline font but lighter and smaller, with purple buttons, 12px corners and outlined cards. The least change from the live site.

Creative directions (F to H)

F. Notebook

Creative. Dot-grid paper, hard offset shadows, mono labels, sticky notes and ticket-stub pricing. Playful but crafted, with a scrolling tape strip at the top.

G. Nightshift studio

Creative. A dark glassy look with glow, gradient text and a live chat demo that types by itself. Shows what a bot does in the first screen.

H. Bento editorial

Creative. Oversized headline type, a bento grid of tiles, a scrolling model strip, big numbered rows and a floating call to action bar.
