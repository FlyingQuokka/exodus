# Affiliate swipe emails, evergreen set, draft 2 (25 November 2026)

Six emails for affiliates to adapt and send to their own lists. They replace the six emails in `affiliate-swipe-material-v1.md`, which followed the older rules (a disclosure line and no emotional angle) and used the old word for credits.

The first drafts came from the Genesis bot `mario-bot-`. I removed everything that claims the affiliate's own experience (for example "I built a bot" or "analytics show me"), because an affiliate who has not done that cannot truthfully send it. I also removed any claim about how a bot behaves that the product facts do not state.

How to use them in the app:
- The only link in each email is `{affiliate_link}`. The app builds the full link from the relative path set on the creative.
- Suggested relative path for each: email 1 `/`, email 2 `/how-it-works`, email 3 `/`, email 4 `/`, email 5 the link from Promote on the listing page (no relative path), email 6 `/pricing`.
- There are no deal terms and no dates, so the set stays valid until the pricing facts change.
- Email 5 has two bracketed parts for the affiliate to fill in: `[BOT NAME]` and `[ONE LINE FROM THE LISTING]`.
