## S28 - Seller profile, badges and a tidy Lab (day 29, Pro and above)

**Subject:** [Botter's Lab] How to be a seller people trust

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday we looked at a free bot as a teaser and a paid bot as the full version. Today, how to become a seller people trust.

New topic: buyers on the Bot Market see your profile and your replies to reviews before they pay. After that comes a short tidy-up so your Lab stays in good order.

**Your profile and badges**

[My profile](https://lab.{{domain}}/market/profile) shows what buyers see about you. Each listing carries About the seller with your badges, your Average rating, your Verified sales and Member since.

Badges are Verified Seller (you've been paid out or set up payouts), 10 Sales (ten verified sales) and Top Rated. A badge shows only while its condition still holds.

**Replying to reviews**

Reviews on your listings are marked Verified purchase. Open [Sales](https://lab.{{domain}}/sales) then select Reply on a review and press Post the reply. Edit the reply changes it later.

Here's a reply you can adapt:

> Thank you for your review. [ONE SENTENCE ABOUT WHAT THEY MENTIONED]. I'm glad you're using the bot and I'll keep improving it.

In [Settings](https://lab.{{domain}}/settings) the Licence terms section shows what buyers agree to. If you change the terms press Notify my buyers and each current licence holder receives a link to the terms page. Saving on its own sends no email.

**Check Sales and Analytics once a week**

Pick one day each week to open Sales and [Analytics](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/analytics). Sales lists each sale with your share and its status. Analytics shows conversations, messages and credits used for each bot.

To have each sale and review reach your inbox switch on Sales under Email notifications in Settings.

**Three tidy-up jobs in Settings**

- Timezone: in Account choose your Timezone and press Save timezone. Dates in Botter's Lab then show in your own timezone.
- Email notifications: Insights emails you when a Conversation insights report is ready and Sales emails you about each sale, review and commission. Press Save after you change them. Receipts, security and plan-change emails are always sent.
- Data export: the zip holds your own bots with their instructions and knowledge files, your conversations, your sales, purchases and commissions, your credit and earnings history and files you uploaded. Integration passwords and keys are never included.

To export press Request export. It shows Being prepared and we email you. When it says Ready press Download before the Available until date. If it shows Failed press Request export again.

**Task for today**

Open My profile and check which badges show. Then save your Timezone in Settings and press Request export so you have a copy.

Keep building trust,

Your Botter Support Team

P.S. Next up: the last email. Tomorrow you're a proper Botter.

**Notes**
- Facts: My profile and badges and replying from Sales (6.8). Licence terms and Notify my buyers (6.7). Sales and Analytics (7.1 and 8.1). Settings sections, Timezone, Email notifications and always-sent emails (12.1). Data export steps and statuses and contents (12.4). Selling is Pro Botter and above.
- Tip 6 (check Sales and Analytics once a week) is the "Check Sales and Analytics" section.
- The Founder badge is not mentioned. Verified revenue is left out. The payment provider is not named.
- The export list says "your own bots" because instructions and files of a bought bot can be left out while its refund window is open.
- Recap suits all Pro and above members because it echoes TIP2 on day 28 and not a track-specific email.
- Data export ships at launch (owner decision 1 November 2026). The app agent confirms it is built for every account with no plan gate.
- Automation: Welcome sequence day 29. Receivers: Pro Botter and Master Botter tags. Lower tiers receive the preview below. Tags tested: tier tag. Welcome Active stays.

## S30-A - You've finished the welcome emails (day 30, Apprentice Botter)

**Subject:** [Botterverse] You've finished the welcome emails

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday we covered your seller profile (or what one looks like) and tidied up Settings.

New topic: this is the last of the welcome emails. It points you to where to find help and to a few features worth another look.

**Where to find help**

Press Help beside any page title in the Botter's Lab. It opens the article for that page in a new tab and all the articles are in the [help articles](https://{{domain}}/docs/).

For anything else use the [Support](https://{{domain}}/support/) link at the foot of the menu. It opens the help desk in a new tab and it has its own sign-in separate from the Botter's Lab. The [Feedback](https://{{domain}}/feedback/) link next to it sends a Bug report or a Feature suggestion.

**Things worth another look**

- [Import a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/import) lets you paste in a bot you built elsewhere. It uses no credits.
- Duplicate at the foot of the Create a bot page copies a working bot so you can try a new idea safely.
- [Browse](https://lab.{{domain}}/market) in the Bot Market has a 4 stars and up filter.
- Conversation insights shows what your bot couldn't answer. It uses credits so switch it on when you need it.

**What your plan can grow into**

Novice Botter adds every model and selling access with a paywall. It also adds integrations, basic analytics and the affiliate programme. If you move up we'll send a short run of emails that shows you each one.

The [Pricing](https://{{domain}}/pricing/) page lists what each plan adds. To change plan open [Billing and credits](https://lab.{{domain}}/billing) then Your plan and press Change plan.

**Task for today**

Press Help beside the title of the page you use most. It's worth knowing what's there.

**That's the end of the welcome emails**

You've built a bot, tested it, shared it and found your way around the Botter's Lab. That's the whole welcome track so there are no more daily emails to follow.

From here you'll hear from us about your account and billing. If you ever need a hand the Botter Support Team is a click away.

Thank you for building with us,

Your Botter Support Team

**Notes**
- Facts: Help beside page titles (1.6 and 13.3). Support opens the help desk in a new tab with its own sign-in (13.1). Feedback types (13.2). Change plan path (10.6). Things worth another look are available on every plan (3.12 and 3.10 and 6.3 and 8.2).
- No "Next up" and no P.S. on purpose: this is the last email so it winds the sequence up. The plan paragraph is one short pointer with no price and no pressure.
- Five versions exist because FluentCRM has no per-tier blocks inside one email. The automation sends the version that matches the member's tier tag (Apprentice or Novice or Expert or Pro or Master). A member who upgrades earlier leaves the welcome track and never reaches this email.
