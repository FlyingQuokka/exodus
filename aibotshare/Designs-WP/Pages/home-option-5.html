Still wondering?

Who pays when people chat with my bot?

You do. Each message uses credits from your balance at its real size. Your plan includes credits each month. You can buy more. Set a daily credit limit on a bot and a message cap on a paid bot so a busy day cannot use up your balance.

Can someone copy my instructions or files?

Every bot is told never to reveal or quote or summarise or translate its instructions or files. No instruction is a perfect lock so keep secrets out of them.

I already have a custom GPT. Do I start again?

No. Paste its instructions and files into Import a bot. It opens as a bot you can edit. It uses no credits. The last step lists anything that could not move.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can brief a new assistant you can write a bot's instructions.

Is there a free trial?

No. There is no free plan or trial. Apprentice is $5 a month and you can cancel from Billing and credits when you like. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.

Will I be locked in?

No. Request an export from Settings. Your bots, knowledge and conversations go with you.

I do not have an audience. Can I still sell?

No plan promises sales. You have four ways to reach people. Share a link. Put the bot on your own site. List it on Bot Market for other Botters. Refer people through the affiliate programme.

What if my bot says something wrong?

Every bot is checked before it can go Public. You write the instructions and choose what it knows. Preview it before anyone else sees it. The Certified page does not vouch for the answers so test yours.

Which plan should I pick?

Start with the function you need. Apprentice builds and shares. Novice adds selling and every model. Pro adds Bot Market selling and the CRM connector. You can change plan from Billing and credits. The bought credits stay.
