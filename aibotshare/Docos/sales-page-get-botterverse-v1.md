## 3. What happened

**Heading:** The switch-off was no surprise. The damage was.

Since mid-August personal accounts have not been able to create or publish new custom GPTs. The ones that exist are being switched off.

You knew platforms change the rules. Every marketer does. But this one took away something you had put your name on.

OpenAI offers a way to move your GPT. Here is what moves.

- Your instructions. They go into a private plugin.
- Your knowledge files. They are copied across.

Here is what does not.

- **Your model.** The one you chose and tuned is gone.
- **Your actions.** You must rebuild them on a server of your own.
- **Your sharing settings.** The new plugin starts private.
- **Your link.** The old one goes dark and nobody is sent on to the new one.

Then there is the workaround. You can share a plain conversation so people can still use your work. It shows them your instructions. The thing you built and sold is now handed to anyone who clicks.

## 4. The moment

**Heading:** Picture the click

Someone on your list opens the email where you recommended it. Maybe you sold it. Maybe you gave it away as a bonus. Maybe it was the centre of your course.

They click the link.

It does not load.

They know nothing about plugins or migrations. They know you told them this was worth their time and it is gone.

They do not write to ask what happened. They just stop opening your emails.

Your JV partner sees the thread. Your affiliates think twice about the next launch. Someone who was about to buy from you remembers that the last thing you recommended broke.

You did not only lose a tool. You lost the one thing you had spent years earning, which is the trust of people who follow your advice.

**Heading:** What it comes down to

You are not really angry with a company. You are angry with yourself for not seeing it. You told your people this was good and put your name on it.

Under the anger sits a worse question. Did I build something or did I run someone else's machine and call it mine?

You thought you were a builder. You were a tenant.

## 5. The mechanism

**Heading:** The Landlord In The Middle

This is not bad luck and it is not one bad company. It is how the arrangement is built.

When a platform owns the link and the user data and the model choice and the infrastructure then you are a contractor on their project. You are paid while the contract holds. When it ends you leave with your tools and nothing else.

Think of a shop. You fitted it out. You hung your sign over the door. You brought the people in. But the building was never yours. When the landlord changes the locks you are on the pavement and the sign stays on the wall.

And here is the cruel part. **The deeper you built the more you lost.**

The builder with one test bot walks away. The builder with fifty bots and a paying customer base is captive. Depth felt like mastery. It was what made you easy to trap.

Every platform has some dependency. That is fine. A dependency is a tool you rely on while you own the output. A trap is a tool that owns the output too.

The fix is not to stop building bots. It is to build them where you hold the things that matter. The link. The model choice. The knowledge. A way to leave.

That is what Botterverse is for.

## 7. What is built

[Siamese Twin: each promise is followed at once by the feature that carries it. Real product facts only. No testimonials exist and none are implied.]

**Heading:** Every answer to the test, in the product

**Could you move your work?** Yes.
Paste your GPT's instructions and upload its files with Import a bot. It opens as a bot you can edit and it uses no credits. The last step lists anything that could not move.

**Could you choose the model?** Yes.
Pick Claude or GPT or Gemini or Grok for each bot. Nothing is picked for you.

**Could you reach them?** From Pro.
Connect your own CRM and receive the details of customers who agree to share them.

**Could you leave?** Yes.
Request an export from Settings. Your bots and knowledge and conversations go with you.

**Is the link yours?** It carries your username.
Every Public bot has its own page with your username in the address. You can also paste one snippet to put the bot on your own site. Visitors are told they are talking to an AI.

**Can you sell?** From Novice.
Set a one-time price. Add a message cap and a top-up if you want. Add a 7 or 14 day money-back guarantee for your buyers if you want. Buyers pay through Stripe. Paying users can leave star ratings.

**Is there a market?** Yes.
On Bot Market members trade bots. Buy on any plan. Sell from Pro as a Transfer (the bot moves to the buyer) or a Multi-sell (every buyer receives a copy and you keep the original). Choose from four licences: Personal use and Resell rights and Master resell rights and PLR.

**Does it look like yours?** From Pro.
Add your own logo and colours and font. Remove the powered-by tag.

**Can it do more than talk?** From Novice.
Connect a bot to another service with a simple form.

**Is it checked?** Every bot.
Every bot is checked before it can go Public. Each has a Certified by Botterverse page that shows the owner and the model and what the owner declared. It shows where a bot comes from. It does not vouch for the answers. Anyone can report a bot without an account.

**I have been burned by a platform. Why would this be different?**
No platform is permanent and we will not tell you this one is. What you can check is what you can take with you. Your instructions and files stay yours. Export is built in. **I do not want to start again.**
You are not starting again. You are moving. If your instructions are written the move is a paste and an upload.

**I am not technical.**
There is no code. If you can brief a new assistant you can write a bot's instructions. Files upload the way you attach them to an email.

**Who pays when people chat with my bot?**
You do. Every message uses credits at its real size. Your plan includes credits each month and you can buy more. Set a daily credit limit on a bot so a busy day cannot empty your balance. Credits you buy never expire. Included credits renew each month.

**I do not have an audience.**
No plan promises sales. You have four ways to reach people. Share a link. Put the bot on your own site. List it on Bot Market if you are on Pro. Refer people and earn commission if you are on Novice or above.

**Can someone copy my instructions?**
Every bot is told never to reveal or quote or summarise or translate its instructions or files. No instruction is a perfect lock so keep secrets out of them.

**Can I sell what I make?**
From Novice you can charge for access. You choose the price. We take a fee on each sale and the pricing page shows it.
