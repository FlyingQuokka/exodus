# Welcome sequence plan, version 4 (fast start)

Approved by the owner on 31 October 2026 (with the notes in section 9). This replaces the order in `01-welcome-sequence-plan-v3.md`. The tier tracks and the stop and tag rules in v3 stand. Nothing is set up in FluentCRM. No emails are rewritten until this plan is approved.

## 1. Aim

A working bot on day 1 for members who already have one. A working shared bot by day 3 for everyone. Then ways to earn from it and then the rest of the Lab. Lessons are short and each one is finished in one sitting.

## 4. How lower tiers see gated emails

For each gated email (M1 to M6 and F1 to F6 and so on) a member below the needed tier receives a **preview version** in the same slot. It has the same parts as every email and these changes:
- The content shows what the feature does by describing the screen a member or buyer sees (a mock paywall page and so on). It is labelled as an example.
- One short paragraph says which plan it belongs to and links to Pricing.
- The Task for today is something the member can do on their own plan that prepares for it (for example write the ten questions their audience asks most and upload them as a knowledge file).
- No pressure and no countdown.

This keeps the upgrade teasers in the sequence without a separate set of emails. The old U1 to U13 emails become these previews and are reused where they fit.

## 5. Website bot emails (W1 to W3)

- **W1: the embed snippet.** Open the bot and find Sharing and embed under Advanced. Copy the Embed snippet and paste it into the page. A new bot has Require visitors to sign up switched on so the page shows a Chat with button until it is switched off. Set a Daily boost limit so a busy page can't use up the balance.
- **W2: bots for a sales page.** An FAQ bot that answers questions about a product or an offer. A product helper that suggests which product or package fits. For each: a worked example for Name and Description and Category and Instructions and Conversation starters and the knowledge file contents. Rule: the bot answers only from the owner's own material and never makes income or results claims.
- **W3: bots by niche.** A recipe bot for a food blog. A glossary or explainer bot for a topic. A companion for a course. Same worked fill-in.

All examples are labelled "for example" and none uses an invented customer or a result.

## 6. Example bot emails (N1 to N6)

Each shows one bot a Botter could build to make money from. Each has a short intro and a table of the field values and a Task for today (build it). Marked with the plan needed to sell it. No income claims.

## 7. Tips (TIP1 and TIP2)

The remaining tips go inside other emails as one paragraph each:
- Turn your best existing material into a knowledge file (B3 and N1).
- Test with ten real customer questions before going Public (B2).
- Make the first conversation starter the one that sells (B1).
- Put the link where people already are: an email signature and a thank-you page and a social bio (B2 and W1).
- Ask three real people to try it before you launch (B2).
- Check Sales and Analytics once a week (S28).

## 10. As built (3 November 2026)

The whole sequence is one automation called A0 Welcome, generated by `tools\make-welcome-funnel.py` into `sitetv-sync\dataunnels0-welcome.json`. It has 70 top-level steps and 51 emails. Each day waits until 09:00 (the site timezone) on the next day, every day of the week. A gated day is a tag check: the full email for the tiers that qualify and the preview for everyone else.
