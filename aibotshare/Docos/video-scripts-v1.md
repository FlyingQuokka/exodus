# Get Botterverse: top-of-page video scripts, draft 1

Six scripts for a short screen recording of Botters Lab. Text for review. Lines in [square brackets] are notes and are not spoken.

**Tone.** Calm and plain. A good share of the audience is English, so nothing shouted and no hype words. The voice explains what is on screen as a helpful colleague would. Each script ends on one quiet line and the button does the selling.

**Length.** 35 to 60 seconds. The voiceover runs at about 2.5 words per second, so 40 seconds is about 100 words.

**Captions.** A video at the top of a page often plays without sound, so every script has a short on-screen caption for each beat. Each caption must make sense on its own.

**Labels.** Every screen label in bold is taken from the member guide digest. If a label has changed in the app since 25 October, tell me and I will update the scripts.

**The verse.** Scripts 1, 5 and 6 open on "Welcome to the Botterverse" and end on "Move your bot to the verse". In between, the world's words (Botter, Botters Lab, Bot Market, the ranks, the verse) are used in ordinary sentences and never explained. Scripts 2 to 4 can do the same: open on the card "Welcome to the Botterverse" and close on "Move your bot to the verse". That adds about four seconds to each.

**Which one I would use.** Script 1 for the sales page, because it matches the page's story (move your GPT and own the link). Script 5 is the best general tour if you want one video for the home page too. Script 6 (list building) may be the strongest single pitch to internet marketers and could take the top spot instead of script 1.

---

## Before recording

**The account.** A Master Botter account so every feature can be shown. The username appears in the share link, so choose one that looks right on screen (for example `demo` or a brand name). Never record a real member's account.

**The demo bot.** Something an internet marketer would build and sell. I suggest **Launch Email Writer**: it writes promotion emails for affiliate launches in the user's own voice. It gives no financial advice so it passes the publish check. The instructions to paste are at the end of this file.

**Numbers on screen.** The local site has almost no real usage. Do not fill the dashboards with made-up sales or revenue, because a viewer would read them as results. Scripts 1 to 4 avoid revenue screens. Script 5 shows the analytics panel briefly with the figures out of shot or blurred in Camtasia.

**The address bar.** The local Lab sits on a `.debbie` address. Either crop the browser bar out in Camtasia or record on the live site once it is up.

**Screen size.** Record at 1920 by 1080 with the browser zoomed to about 125% so text reads on a phone.

**Pace.** I will pause for about one second after each click so you have room to cut in Camtasia.

## Prop: the demo bot's instructions

[Pasted on screen in scripts 1 and 5. Kept short so it reads in a frame. Change the voice to suit.]

> You are Launch Email Writer. You help affiliates write promotion emails for product launches.
> Ask for the product name, the launch date, the main benefit and the reader's biggest problem.
> Write in a friendly, plain voice. Short sentences. No hype words and no income claims.
> Give three subject lines and one email of about 200 words.
> If you do not know a fact about the product, ask. Never make one up.
