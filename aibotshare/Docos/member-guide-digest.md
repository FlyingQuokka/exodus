# Botterverse member guide: digest for copywriters

Source: `member-guide.docx` (Botter's Lab from sign-up to selling, as it stands on 25 October 2026). The guide is the source of truth for what the app does. Where it differs from `Handover-WP-Design.md` (section 5) or `botterverse-brief.md`, section 4 below lists the difference and a suggested resolution.

Conventions in this file: screen names, buttons and field labels are in bold exactly as the guide has them. "Guide is silent" means the guide gives no answer. The guide deliberately never prints prices, fees, limits or periods (staff can change them), so every figure comes from the handover or the brief and is marked as such.

---

## 1. Chapter digest

### Chapter 1. Overview

**What a member can do (1.1).** Build a bot from their own instructions and files on the AI model they choose. Share it by a link or put it on their own website. Sell access behind a one-time paywall. Trade bots on **Bot Market**. Refer new members through the affiliate programme.

**Who it is for (1.2).** Coaches, consultants, course creators, small businesses and anyone with an audience. No code. Customers reach the bot through the member's link or website. **Bot Market** is for members only and is not a public store. Every bot is checked before it can go Public. Some kinds are banned. Others are allowed but must say what they do on the **Certified by Botterverse** page.

**Credits (1.3).** Every bot message uses credits. The bot owner pays for visitor and customer chats and for their own testing. Plans include credits each month.

**Plans (1.4).** Five plans. No free plan and no free trial. Each plan includes everything in the one before.

| Plan | What the guide says it adds |
|---|---|
| Apprentice Botter | Building bots and sharing by link or embed. Everyday AI models only (the most expensive model from each provider is not offered). Buying on **Bot Market**. Default look on bot pages |
| Novice Botter | More bots. Every AI model. The paywall with message top-ups. Integrations. Basic analytics. The affiliate programme |
| Expert Botter | More bots and larger included credits. Deeper analytics: category benchmarks, busiest hours and top conversation starters |
| Pro Botter | More bots again. Bot styles with a powered-by tag that can be switched off. A lower platform fee. Selling on **Bot Market** with the seller's own licence terms. The CRM connector. Priority support |
| Master Botter | A/B tests. Advanced analytics (views set against purchases) |

- Where a plan is shown: the plan name sits under the member's name at the top right (for example "Pro Botter").
- A page the plan does not include shows a short note saying what the feature does and which plans include it with a **Change plan** button (or **See your plan** where the upgrade checkout is not set up). Exceptions: the Bot builder's **Style** and **Interest tags** sections are not shown at all below Pro Botter.
- The **Dashboard** shows the bot limit under **Active bots**.
- Included credits expire at the next renewal or plan change. Bought credits never expire. An annual plan still adds credits monthly.
- Buying: plans are bought on the pricing page of the website. The member registers for Botter's Lab with the email address they bought with (before or after buying). After buying, one email arrives with two links: the first sets the password of the billing login (invoices, card changes, cancelling) and the second creates the Botter's Lab account. A member who already has a Botter's Lab account under that email signs in and verifies their email and the plan is added automatically.

**Menu map (1.5).** Left-hand menu (folded behind the menu button on a phone). Groups start folded except the one holding the current page.

| Group | Entries |
|---|---|
| (top) | **Dashboard** |
| Bots | **Bot list**, **Import a bot**, **Analytics**, **A/B tests** (Master Botter), **Bot styles** (Pro Botter and above) |
| Bot Market | **Browse**, **Bots I bought**, **My listings** (Pro Botter and above), **My profile** |
| Earnings | **Sales**, **Payouts** |
| Account | **Billing and credits** |
| (foot) | **Settings**, **Support** (new tab), **Help** (new tab), **Feedback** (new tab), **Affiliates** (Novice Botter and above) |

**Behaviour (1.6).**
- Top bar: page title with a **Help** link. Right side: a red status pill (only when the member must act, for example **Payment past due** or **Plan cancelling**) then the credits pill (balance in whole credits, opens **Billing and credits**) then the theme button (light **Pleasant Pink** and dark **Nightshift**) then the name and plan menu (**Settings**, **Billing and credits**, **Sign out**).
- Edits are kept only on **Save**. A few actions say "takes effect now".
- Dates show in the member's own timezone (set in **Settings**).
- Leaving a form with unsaved changes asks for confirmation.
- Success and information messages close after 5 seconds and warnings and errors after 10.
- Help links open in a new tab. Terms and the Privacy policy are linked in the page foot.

**Quick win.** Press the theme button to switch between **Pleasant Pink** and **Nightshift**.

### Chapter 2. Signing up and the dashboard

**Creating an account (2.1).** One account does building, buying and selling. On the **Create your account** page: **Username** (becomes part of the url the member's bots are served at and the sign-in name, cannot be changed), **First name**, **Last name**, **Email address**, **Password**, **Password again**, **Country** (a note appears if earnings cannot yet be paid to that country - the member can still register, build bots and buy), the terms and privacy tick box, then **Create my account**. A visitor who signs up from a bot's page may see a box asking whether to share their name and email address with the bot's owner.

**Confirming email (2.2).** A link is emailed. The page **Please confirm your email address** says some things stay closed until it is opened (for example making a bot Public or submitting a listing). **Send it again** sends a fresh link.

**Choosing a plan (2.3).** Every plan is a paid subscription bought on the website pricing page with the same email as the account. If the member paid before having an account the banner **Verify your email to activate your plan** shows until they confirm (offers **Send the link again**). With no plan, **Your plan** on **Billing and credits** says **You have no plan yet** and offers **See the plans**.

**Signing in (2.4).** The **Sign in** page takes **Username** and **Password**, never the email address. **Keep me signed in** is for a trusted device. **Forgotten username?** (enter the email, press **Send me my username**) and **Forgotten password?** (enter the username, press **Send me a link**).

**Unserved countries (2.5).** Payments are not taken from some countries, "the EU among them". This covers plans, credits, bot access and **Bot Market** purchases. A visitor sees **Not available in your country** at a pay step. Free Public bots still work for them.

**Dashboard (2.6).** First page after sign-in. Welcome line plus: **Credit balance**, **Usage this month**, **Active bots** (for example "3 of 5", with paused count - paused bots count towards the limit), **Revenue over time** (the member's share of sales across all bots; **Date range** offers **Last week**, **Last month**, **Year to date**, **Last 5 years**, **Custom range**) and **Your bots** (each with status such as **Running** or **Paused by you**, selecting one opens **Bot analytics**). With no bots the page offers **New bot**. If the plan has no room it offers **Change plan**.

**Quick win.** Click **Keep me signed in** on a trusted device. Confirm the email address from the first email.

### Chapter 3. Building a bot

**Creating (3.1).** A new bot starts **Personal**. **Bot list**, then **New bot**, then **Details** (**Name** required, **Description** and **Image** optional), then **Instructions**, then **Save**. Knowledge files, integrations, mode and preview only work once the bot is saved. Every bot (Personal or Public, running or paused, built or bought) counts towards the plan limit. At the limit **New bot** is hidden and a note says **You are at your plan's bot limit. Change your plan to add more bots.**

**Bot builder sections (3.2).** One page. Every section starts folded. A section list sits beside them on a wide screen and a **Jump to** menu on a phone.

1 **Details** (jpg, png or webp image) / 2 **Category** / 3 **Instructions** / 4 **Conversation starters** / 5 **Knowledge files** / 6 **Model** / 7 **Mode** / 8 **Paywall and pricing** / 9 **Style** (Pro Botter and above) / 10 **Integrations** / 11 **Sharing and embed** (Advanced) / 12 **Interest tags** (Advanced, Pro Botter and above) / 13 **Preview** (Advanced) / 14 **Analytics** (Advanced). **Duplicate**, **Pause** or **Unpause** and **Delete** sit at the foot.

Bought bots on **Personal use**, **Resell rights** or **Master resell rights** hide **Instructions** and **Knowledge files**. A PLR bot can be renamed and edited.

**Saving (3.3).** The **Save** bar at the foot holds the **Save** button only. Pressing it shows **The bot is saved.** Saved: name, description, image, category, instructions, starters, model, paywall settings, style, **Require visitors to sign up**, **Daily credit limit** and the **Conversation insights** switch. Takes effect now without **Save**: **Make Public** or **Switch back to Personal**, **Confirm declarations**, uploading and deleting a knowledge file, integration changes and **Duplicate**, **Pause**, **Unpause**, **Delete**. Every saved change to a Public bot runs the safety check again.

**Category (3.4).** Choose from the **Category** list (none is allowed for a bot not for sale) or press **Suggest a category** ("Suggested: Marketing. Save to keep it."). Suggestions use no credits. A bot needs a category before it can be listed.

**Instructions (3.5).** Plain language box **What the bot should do**. The guide's checklist: who the bot is for and what it helps with, tone, what it should not do or discuss, how to use its knowledge files and what to say when it does not know. If too long, **Save** refuses: **The instructions are too large for this model**. Every bot is told never to reveal, quote, summarise or translate its instructions or knowledge files. Each save keeps a new version of the instructions.

**Conversation starters (3.6).** **Add a starter**, **Remove**, **Save**. Visitors see them on opening the bot. They also show on the paywall under **Things you can ask it**. **Top conversation starters** on **Bot analytics** counts them (Expert Botter and above).

**Knowledge files (3.7).** Types: PDF, Word, text, Markdown and CSV. Uploading uses no credits. Save the bot first. **Add a file**, **Browse**, **Upload**. Status **Being read** then **Ready** (only ready files are used). Errors: **This file could not be read.** Delete is permanent after a confirmation. When knowledge fits, all of it is sent with each message. When it does not, the best-matching passages are sent. Number and size limits are shown on screen. The guide gives no figures.

**Model (3.8).** Four families: Claude (default), GPT, Gemini and Grok. Apprentice Botter: everyday models. Novice Botter and above: every model. A dearer model uses more credits per message. Refusals: **The instructions are too large for this model**, **This model is not available to you. Choose another model.** A model can be withdrawn for new bots while existing bots keep working. A fully retired model means the bot stops answering until the owner picks another. On a Public bot a model change means confirming declarations again.

**Preview (3.9).** **Preview**, under **Advanced**. Uses last saved version. Uses the member's credits. Visitor limits do not apply to the owner. Works on a Personal bot.

**Duplicate, pause, delete (3.10).** **Duplicate** adds "Copy" to the name and the copy starts Personal with its paywall off (keeps instructions, files, starters, model and integrations with credentials). It counts towards the limit and is hidden at the limit. A bought bot cannot be duplicated until its refund window has passed or it has been put on sale. **Pause** and **Unpause**. Other statuses: **Paused by the Botter Support Team** (only staff can unpause), **Paused by your plan**, **Paused for account closing**. **Delete** opens **Delete this bot?** with **Delete bot** and **Keep it**. A never-shared bot is erased. A shared, listed or paid-for bot is removed from the list and kept as a record with paying customers refunded the unused part from the owner's earnings.

**Bot list (3.11).** **Search your bots**, a status filter (**Any status**, **Paused for any reason**) and a mode filter (**Any mode**, **Personal**, **Public**). Statuses: **Running**, **Paused by you**, **Paused by the Botter Support Team**, **Paused for account closing**, **Paused by your plan**. Bought bots are marked **Purchased**. When the plan drops, bots over the limit are paused newest first and **Keep this bot** chooses which run.

**Importing (3.12).** **Import a bot**. Pasting only: it does not connect to any other account (the guide's example source is "a custom GPT"). No credits. Nothing is saved until the last step. Steps: **Instructions** (paste, **Continue**), **Files** (**Browse**), **Integrations** (optional, **Add integration**), **Review** (suggested **Name**, **Description**, **Category**, **Starters**; **Not carried across** lists what could not move), **Create bot**. Opens as Personal in the builder. Integrations are not part of Apprentice Botter (step says so and the member can carry on).

**Quick wins.** **Suggest a category** (no credits). **Import a bot** (no credits). **Duplicate** a working bot to test an idea. **Preview** a message before anyone else sees the bot.

### Chapter 4. Publishing and sharing

**Personal and Public (4.1).** Personal: owner only, no share link, no embed, no customers, cannot be listed. Can stay Personal for ever. Public: sharing, embedding, selling and customers. Needs confirmed email and accepted latest terms and confirmed declarations. Steps: **Mode**, tick declarations, **Confirm declarations**, **Make Public** (button shows **Checking**; may say **Still checking** - reload later). **Switch back to Personal** stops link, embed and customers at once and hides any live listing. Refusal messages: personal-licence bots stay private, **Confirm this bot's declarations, above the Make Public button, before you make it Public.**, open refund request, **Confirm your email address before you make a bot Public**, **Review the terms**. Making a bought bot Public ends the refund right and asks for a confirmation.

**Declarations (4.2).** Seven (full wording in the table in the guide): 1 collects personal details / 2 special category data / 3 financial or payment details / 4 under-18s / 5 regulated advice (medical, legal, financial, insurance) / 6 automated decisions that materially affect a person / 7 sends data to third parties beyond the model provider. They show on the **Certified by Botterverse** page. A declaration does not stop a bot going Public. A false one breaks the terms. If the check finds one it ticks and locks it ("Found and locked by our check"). Re-confirmation is needed after a model change, integration change, knowledge change or a new finding. The bot stays Public meanwhile. If not confirmed in time it switches to Personal and the member is emailed. A bought bot arrives with declarations cleared.

**Publish check (4.3).** Checks name, description, instructions and knowledge files. Not allowed: sexual content / gambling and betting / illegal activity / fraud, scams or malicious software / harm to children or anyone else / hate and harassment / impersonating a real person or organisation / misleading financial or health claims / covert monitoring of people / spam, fake reviews or political manipulation / passing off work as someone's own for academic credit. Full list is in the terms. Outcomes: passes (Public), **The check refused this bot.** (stays Personal and names the categories), a category that goes to staff (goes Public and staff look), a declaration applies (ticked and locked), model provider refused text (Personal, reason shown, member emailed). After go-live a small share of conversations is checked after the fact and a visitor's chat is never interrupted. Disputes go through Support.

**Share link (4.4).** Each Public bot has its own page on an address starting with the member's username. **Sharing and embed**, under **Advanced**, copy button beside **Share link** ("Copied"). **Require visitors to sign up** (on: visitors sign up or sign in first so the owner knows who is chatting and with the CRM connector can reach them later; off: anyone chats at once; a paid bot always needs an account). **Daily credit limit**: empty uses the platform default or enter whole credits. At the limit free visitors are told the bot has reached its limit for the day and paid buyers and the owner carry on. Resets at midnight UTC and the owner is emailed once that day. Visitor limits (messages per minute and per day) are the same for every bot and do not apply to the owner. A bot that cannot answer tells the visitor why.

**Embed (4.5).** Copy button beside **Embed snippet** and paste into the site's custom code or HTML box. **Require visitors to sign up** is on for a new bot and then the embed shows a button not the chat. Help text: "Turn off to allow users to chat with your bot embedded in your site." Free bot with sign-up off: chat runs in the page. Paid bot or sign-up on: a button "Chat with" plus the bot name that opens the bot's page in a new tab. The embed follows the bot when it changes. Visitors are told they are talking to an AI assistant before the first reply. The footer carries **Report this bot**, **Certified by Botterverse** and the powered-by tag unless a style removes it. Switching to Personal stops the embed.

**Bot styles (4.6).** Pro Botter and above. Affects the chat page, paywall, embed and sign-up pages for the bot, not Botter's Lab. **Bot styles**, **New style**, cards: Name, Branding (logo jpg/png/webp, **Display name**), Colours (**Background**, **Bot message bubble**, **Customer message bubble**, **Text**, **Accent**), Text and corners (**Font**, **Chat text size** Small/Medium/Large, **Corners** Square/Soft/Round), What shows (**Show the header**, **Show the bot's image**, **Show the powered-by tag**). Live Preview. Contrast warnings. Apply with **Style** in the builder or **Apply to another bot**. **Edit** changes every bot on the style and **Duplicate as a new style** copies it. The powered-by tag carries the owner's affiliate link and earns only once they have joined the programme. Below Pro Botter pages revert to the default look with the tag shown and styles are kept.

**Certified by Botterverse and Report this bot (4.7).** Both appear on every chat page, paywall, embed and listing and cannot be switched off. The Certified page shows: registered on Botterverse, owner's username and member length, model, all seven declarations with who set each, explanations and third parties, last screened, how long messages are kept (by Botterverse and the model provider) and active/paused/suspended. It certifies where a bot comes from and what the owner declared and does not certify accuracy, safety or fitness. **Report this bot**: anyone, no account: **Reason**, **What is wrong**, **Your email address** (optional), **Send the report**.

**In short (4.8).** The guide's path from first bot to Public is reproduced in section 2 below.

**Quick wins.** Copy the **Share link** once the bot is Public. Send a Personal bot's **Preview** a question it should refuse.

### Chapter 5. The paywall and selling access

**How it works (5.1).** Novice Botter and above. Visitors pay a one-time price. A purchase is called an access and can include a set number of messages with top-ups. No subscription. Each sale adds to earnings less the plan's platform fee.

**Switching on (5.2).** Public bot, **Paywall and pricing**, **Sell access to this bot**, **One-time price**, optional **Message cap** and top-up, **Money-back guarantee**, **Show the bot's rating on its paywall page**, **Save**. A bought bot asks for confirmation that this ends its refund right. A bot on **Personal use** cannot have a paywall.

**Fields (5.3).** **One-time price** (US dollars), **Message cap** (empty means no cap), **Top-up size in messages** and **Top-up price**. A top-up needs both fields and a cap. Minimum prices exist (guide gives no figure). Price changes apply to new buyers. The platform fee is taken from each access and each top-up. The cap protects the owner because each buyer message is paid from the owner's credits. At the cap the buyer sees **Top up**.

**Guarantee (5.4).** **No guarantee**, **7 days** or **14 days** (only periods within the platform refund period are offered). Fixed wording on the paywall. A longer promise made elsewhere may leave the owner unable to refund from earnings because of the holdback.

**What buyers see (5.5).** Paywall shows name, rating, description, **Things you can ask it**, price, messages, top-up and guarantee. Signed-out visitor: **Sign up** or **Sign in**. Buyer ticks an agreement (access starts at once, refund only under the owner's guarantee), presses **Pay** and the chat opens. **Bots I bought** shows messages left (or **Unlimited messages**), **Open chat**, a button such as "Top up 50 messages for $9.00" and **Money-back guarantee** until a date. **Request a refund** asks **Why are you asking for a refund?** and **Send the claim**. **Rate this bot**: 1 to 5 stars after a few messages.

**Handling a claim (5.6).** Owner is emailed. **Sales** shows the claim, reason and automatic refund date. The owner can press **Refund** or **Ask the Botter Support Team to review**. Otherwise it is refunded automatically. A guarantee refund takes the full price from earnings even below zero. Deleting a paid-for bot or closing the account refunds unused messages from earnings.

**If the plan changes (5.7).** Below Novice Botter the paywall closes to new buyers. Existing buyers keep access.

**Quick win.** Open the **Share link** in a private browser window to see the paywall as a buyer does.

### Chapter 6. Bot Market

**What it is (6.1).** Members sell bots to each other. The buyer receives the bot itself and runs it on their own credits. Every plan can buy. Selling is Pro Botter and above.

**Sale types (6.2).** **Transfer** (ownership moves, sells once) and **Multi-sell** (each buyer receives their own copy with the listing's licence and the seller keeps the original). A buyer keeps the version they bought and later updates do not reach earlier buyers. Licences: **Personal use**, **Resell rights**, **Master resell rights**, **PLR** (what each allows is in the guide's table). A bot the member built carries no licence and can be listed on any of the four. Botterverse sells as seller of record and the seller grants the licence under their own terms. Integration passwords and keys never move and a bought bot arrives with integrations switched off. A transferred Public bot keeps serving its customers on the buyer's credits and its old address forwards.

**Browsing and buying (6.3).** **Browse**, **Search Bot Market**, filters **Any category**, **Any model**, **Any sale type**, **Any owner**, **Any release date**, **Includes integrations**, **4 stars and up**. Sort: **Relevance**, **Top rated**, **Price high to low**, **Price low to high**. Cards show name, rating, price, description, category, model and an **Integrations** mark. Listing pages show **Reviews** (**Verified purchase**), **About the seller** (badges, **Average rating**, **Verified sales**, **Member since**; optionally **Verified conversations** and **Verified revenue**) and strips **Customers who also bought**, **You might also like**, **More from this seller**. Buy: **Buy**, read **The licence you are buying**, tick the delivery and refund box, tick **I accept the seller's licence terms shown above.**, **Pay**. Then **Confirming your payment** and the bot appears in **Bot list** as **Purchased** and the buyer is emailed. **Checkout cancelled** confirms nothing was charged. Refusals: own listing, plan bot limit, **Purchases are not available in your country.**, **This listing is not available.** A payment that can no longer be delivered is refunded in full (**Your payment has been refunded**).

**Bought bots (6.4).** Belong to the buyer and run on their credits. Before using with customers: enter integration credentials and switch each on then confirm declarations. **Licence terms**, **Request a refund** (**Why are you asking for a refund?**, **Send the request**), **Leave a review** (**Your rating** 1 to 5, **Your review** optional, **Publish the review**). Bot Market refunds are decided by the Botter Support Team only when the bot does not work or is not as described. An approved refund is the price paid less a processing fee. Putting a bot on sale ends the right to a refund. A PLR bot must be renamed before listing.

**Listing (6.5).** Pro Botter and above. Public bot, declarations confirmed, category chosen, licence terms saved, email confirmed. **My listings**, **List a bot for sale**, **List it**, **Listing details** (**Description**, **Tags**, **Suggest a description and tags** with no credits), **Sale type and licence**, **Price** (**Price (USD)**, **Affiliate share (%)**), platform fee line, **On the listing** switches (**Show verified sales and conversations**, **Show verified revenue**, **Show in Bot Market**, **Require attribution on copies**), **Submit** or **Save as draft**. Statuses: **Draft**, **In review**, **Live**, **Sold**, **Removed**, **Hidden while closing**. Seller is emailed when a listing goes live. Edit, **Unlist** and **View** exist.

**Blocked listings (6.6).** The form states the reason at the top and under **Submit is not available yet**. The full reason list is in the guide (licence terms, declarations, category, PLR rename, terms acceptance, email, open refund request, not Public, one listing per bot, plan). A **Live** listing can still be hidden when the bot is Personal or paused or the plan drops below Pro Botter or the account is suspended or staff reject the licence terms.

**Licence terms (6.7).** **Settings**, **Licence terms** (Pro Botter and above). **Copy our template** (one-off) or **Your licence terms**, **Save**. Saved terms are checked. Botterverse gives no legal advice about them. **Notify my buyers** emails each current licence holder a link to the terms page.

**Seller profile and badges (6.8).** **My profile**. Badges: **Founder** (the member's subscription uses the founders coupon), **Verified Seller** (paid out or payouts set up), **10 Sales** (ten verified sales), **Top Rated**. Badges show only while their condition holds. Sellers reply to reviews from **Sales** with **Reply**, **Post the reply**, **Edit the reply**.

**Quick wins.** **Browse** with the **4 stars and up** filter. Use **Suggest a description and tags** when listing.

### Chapter 7. Earnings and payouts

**Sales (7.1).** **Sales** lists every paywall access, message top-up and **Bot Market** sale. Columns: **Bot and type**, **Buyer and date** (the buyer's username), **Price**, **Platform fee**, **Your share**, **Status** (**Paid**, **Partly refunded**, **Refunded**, **Disputed**, **Pending**). Actions: **Refund**, **Ask the Botter Support Team to review**, **Comment**, **Report a licence breach**, **Reply**. Optional email for each sale, review and commission: switch on **Sales** under **Email notifications** in **Settings**.

**Earnings and holds (7.2).** Botterverse takes every payment as seller of record and pays the member their share on withdrawal. **Payouts** shows **Your earnings**: **Available to withdraw**, **Held for refunds** (each amount shows **Withdrawable from** a date or **Held until its dispute or refund request closes**). Promoted listing commission waits the same way. Subscription commissions are added only after the refund period so they are withdrawable at once. **Customer hold** applies if a paid-for bot stops serving. A refund after withdrawal can take earnings below zero. Recovery comes from future earnings and money already in the bank is never taken back.

**Refunds and disputes (7.3).** Seller refunds a paywall access or top-up from **Sales** within the refund period (**Refunded to the buyer**, **Covered by your earnings**, **Confirm the refund**). The platform keeps its fee. A shortfall can be paid by card (**Shortfall**, **Card processing fee**, **You pay by card**). Sellers cannot refund **Bot Market** sales. The buyer asks Support. The seller may use **Comment** and **Save the comment** and that sale's share is held meanwhile. Disputes: **Disputed**, share held, seller emailed, a lost dispute counts as a refund and dispute fees come from earnings. **Report a licence breach**: **What has the buyer done outside the licence?**, **Evidence**, **Send the report**. An upheld breach revokes the licence and removes the bot with no buyer refund.

**Setting up payouts (7.4).** Optional until earnings reach the smallest withdrawal (the guide gives no figure). **Payouts**, **Your country**, **Set up payouts**, Stripe's own form. Messages: **Payouts are set up**, **Finish entering your bank details on Stripe's form.**, **The Botter Support Team is setting up your payouts and will email you once it is done.** (some are set up by hand), **Stripe can no longer pay your bank account. Update your bank details to withdraw again.** A note appears when Stripe cannot yet pay the country, with nothing blocked and earnings still building. Botterverse never sees bank details.

**Withdrawing (7.5).** **Withdraw** card: **Amount**, **Show the fees** (**Withdrawal**, Stripe's payout fees, **Exchange rate**, **What will arrive**), **Confirm the withdrawal**, **Change the amount**. Fees are passed on at cost. **Withdrawals** statuses: **Requested** (some are sent on set days), **Being sent by the Botter Support Team** or **Being processed by the Botter Support Team** (staff send by hand), **Sent** (member emailed), **Paid into your bank account**, **Failed. Check your bank details** or **Returned by your bank. Check your bank details** (amount returns to earnings), **Cancelled**. **Cancel** then **Cancel the withdrawal** while **Requested**.

**Quick win.** Switch on **Sales** under **Email notifications** in **Settings** so each sale and review reaches the inbox.

### Chapter 8. Analytics and testing

**Bot analytics (8.1).** Opens from **Analytics**, a bot on the **Dashboard** or **Full analytics** in the builder. **Bot** and **Date range** controls. Every figure has an info mark.
- Basic (Novice Botter and above): conversations with change against the previous period, messages with messages per conversation, credits used with credits per message, revenue with number of access purchases, **Conversations over time**.
- Deeper (Expert Botter and above): **Category benchmarks** (category average for messages per buyer, price and conversion beside **This bot**, hidden until enough bots feed it), **Busiest hours**, **Top conversation starters**.
- Advanced (Master Botter): **Conversion**: **Paywall views**, **Listing views**, **Purchases**, **Conversion**.
- Apprentice Botter: no panels apart from conversation insights if switched on.

**Conversation insights (8.2).** Off until switched on. No plan includes them so each run costs the owner credits. **Analytics** under **Advanced** in the builder, **Conversation insights** switch, **Save**. Panel shows **Unanswered questions**, **Common topics**, **Suggested additions** (**Add to knowledge**, **Add to instructions**). Optional email when a report is ready (**Insights** under **Email notifications**). Nothing is charged for a skipped or failed run. Not offered on bots whose instructions the owner cannot see.

**A/B tests (8.3).** Master Botter only. The bot as it stands is the control. Price is never tested. **A/B tests**, **Bot**, **Control's share (%)**, per variant **Variant name**, **Share (%)**, **Instructions**, **Starters**, **Add a variant** (shares total 100%), **Save** (Draft), **Start the test**. While **Running** the same visitor always sees the same version. **Results**: **Conversations**, **Messages per conversation**, **Returning customers**, **Paywall conversion**, **Revenue per conversation**. Finish: **Choose as winner**, **Keep the control**, **Stop the test**, **Start a new test**. A test stops by itself if the bot is sold by Transfer or deleted or the plan drops below Master Botter.

**Quick win.** Hover or tap the info mark beside a figure on **Bot analytics**.

### Chapter 9. Integrations and the CRM

**Integrations (9.1).** Novice Botter and above. A bot calls one of the member's own services while chatting (a booking system, an order lookup or a price list). Save the bot first. **Integrations**, **Add an integration**, **Name**, **Method**, **What it does**, **Endpoint address** (must start with https), **Add a parameter** (**Name**, **Description**, **Type**, **Sent in**, **Default value**, **Required**), **Authentication** (**None**, **Bearer token**, **API key in a header**, **Basic (user name and password)**), **Switched on**, **Save integration**. Credentials are stored encrypted and never shown again. Changes take effect now. Data sent to anyone other than the model provider must be declared under declaration 7.

**CRM connector (9.2).** Pro Botter and above. **Settings**, **CRM**. Sends customers' details and actions to the member's own CRM by webhook, once the customer agrees. **Connection**: **Webhook address** (https) and **Your privacy notice** (required address). **Save** then **Your signing secret** (shown once) then **Send test event** then **Switch on**. Controls: **Switch off**, **Switch on**, **Replace secret**, **Disconnect**. After repeated failures the connection switches off and the member is emailed. Each event holds: event, tag, name and email address of the customer, bot name and slug, A/B variant, time and event id. The member becomes responsible for the data under data protection law and must honour every unsubscribe. Technical signing detail is for the member's developer (X-ABS-Signature, HMAC-SHA256).

**Lifecycle tags (9.3).** Member-set tags per event, sent to the member's CRM. Groups and events: Lead (signed up to a bot / first conversation started / signed up but never chatted), Engaged (reached a number of messages / came back after going quiet), Free user ready to buy (hit the free daily limit), Buyer (bought access / bought again / refunded), Upsell timing (allowance running low / allowance used up), Re-engage (went quiet), Feedback (rated 4 or more stars / rated 2 or fewer stars). Thresholds are set by the member. `{bot}` in a tag fills with the bot slug. A blank tag means that event is not sent.

**Interest tags (9.4).** Keyword rules, whole-word match, case-insensitive, customer messages only (never the bot's replies), one send per tag per customer. **Settings**, **CRM**, **Interest tags**, **Add a rule**, **Tag**, **Keywords or phrases**, **Cover all my bots**, **Bots this rule covers**, **Save**. Rule limits apply per plan (the guide gives no figure) and rules over the limit are paused with **Paused because your plan allows fewer rules**. Sent only for customers who agreed to share details.

**Consent and the customer CSV (9.5).** Nothing is sent to the CRM unless the customer agrees. Customers without an account are never sent. While the connection is on, an unticked box asks the customer to send their name and email address to the owner. It shows the owner's name and username and a link to their privacy notice. It appears at account creation from the owner's bot page, on the paywall and once in the chat for a signed-in customer not asked before. It is never required. Customers stop sharing under **Shared with bot owners** in their own **Settings** (nothing new is sent and removal of earlier data means asking the owner directly). **Settings**, **CRM**, **Your customers**, **Download customers (CSV)**: lists every customer who agreed and has not stopped, with name, email address, the bot, where they agreed and the date.

**Quick win.** **Send test event** after saving the webhook.

### Chapter 10. Credits and billing

**Credits (10.1).** Each message uses credits by its real size. A more expensive model uses more. The member pays for visitor and customer chats and own preview testing and conversation insights if on. Uploading knowledge files, the importer, category suggestions and listing suggestions use no credits. Sources: plan credits, credit packs, auto top-up, promo codes. **Billing and credits** has five sections in order: **Credits and usage**, **Auto top-up**, **Promo code**, **Buy credits**, **Your plan**. Credits left, the usage graph and history labels (**Messages**, **Plan credits**, **Credits bought**, **Promo code**, **Added by the Botter Support Team**, **Refunded purchase**, **Adjustment**). The member is emailed when credits fall below their auto top-up threshold and again when credits cannot cover a message. At that point all of the owner's bots stop answering everyone, including buyers, until credits are added.

**Whole credits (10.2).** The app tracks four decimal places. Displays round down to whole credits.

**Credit packs (10.3).** **Billing and credits**, **Buy credits**, **Buy** beside a pack, checkout opens in a new tab. Credits are added when payment goes through. Buy with the account email. A pack shows total credits and how many are bonus credits. A refunded pack's credits are removed up to the current balance. If no packs are on sale the section points to auto top-up. Pack names and prices are not in the guide (handover section 5 has them).

**Auto top-up (10.4).** Off until switched on. **Agree and switch on** after reading the statement (link: **The Auto top-up section of our terms and conditions**). **Top up when my credits fall below** (whole credits) and **Top-up amount** (dollars, one credit per cent, no pack bonus). Needs an active plan and a card on file. Emailed on every charge. A daily cap pauses it until the next day with an email. If the bank asks for approval it is not retried (buy a pack instead). A declined card is tried once more and then auto top-up switches off with an email. It also switches off on **Switch off**, plan end and account closure. Reopening leaves it off. A changed statement pauses it until the member agrees again, with an email.

**Promo codes (10.5).** Add bonus credits only and never change a price. **Promo code**, **Redeem**. Plan discounts are a separate coupon entered at the cart checkout.

**Changing plan (10.6).** **Billing and credits**, **Your plan**, **Change your plan**, **Change plan**. Opens the upgrade checkout in a new tab with a prorated amount. Cheaper monthly plan part way through the month loses the unused part. Annual to monthly or to a cheaper annual plan refunds each whole unused month after the current one. The new plan's first payment replaces unused included credits and bought credits stay. Moving down: bots over the limit are paused newest first (**Keep this bot**), below Pro Botter listings are hidden and bot pages go to the default style, below Novice Botter paywalls close to new buyers, moving to Apprentice leaves the affiliate programme (earned commission unaffected), below Master Botter A/B tests stop. Moving back up restores them. **Plan changes are not available here yet.** means the member contacts the Botter Support Team.

**Cancelling (10.7).** **Cancel my plan** (press twice, **Go back** keeps it). Monthly: plan runs to end of the paid period. Annual: plan ends at the end of the current month and whole unused months are refunded to the card (months and amount are shown first). Until the end: **Ends** with a date and **Keep my plan**. After the plan ends: account stays open, can sign in and chat on bots bought access to, own bots pause with data kept, unused plan credits expire, bought credits stay and auto top-up switches off.

**Invoices (10.8).** **Open the customer area** (under **Invoices, receipts and saved cards**). The billing login is separate from Botter's Lab. Its set-password link works once and expires after 24 hours. **Forgotten password?** on the billing sign-in page sends **Send me a link**. A member with an existing help desk sign-in under that email has no billing login created and uses the help desk sign-in. Receipts go to the Botter's Lab account email. Bot access, message top-ups and **Bot Market** purchases are single payments that each send their own receipt and save no card.

**Quick wins.** Redeem a promo code (credits show at once). Switch on **Auto top-up**. Press the credits pill.

### Chapter 11. Referring (affiliates)

Commission is earned on subscription payments (first payment and every renewal) of members who joined through the link and on **Bot Market** sales through the member's listing links (at the seller's share). **Commissions (11.4).** First ever payment earns a larger share and every later payment earns a smaller one. The guide gives no rates. Support can give the current rates. A commission waits for the refund period. Botterverse reviews and pays it into earnings as withdrawable. **Subscription commissions** statuses: **Pending**, **Paid**, **Skipped**, **Under review**, **Not paid: self-referral**. After grace: **The grace period for this account has ended**, a new account and username are needed.

**Privacy (12.7).** Terms and privacy policy are readable without an account. Banner asks for **Accept** on change (until then no publishing or listing). Member decides what a bot collects via declarations. Card details are entered on the payment processor's pages and bank details on Stripe's own form (Botterverse sees neither).

**Quick wins.** **Request export**. **Save timezone**.
