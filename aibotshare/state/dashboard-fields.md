## Core Offer

Botterverse lets non-developer Botters build AI bots on Claude, ChatGPT, Grok or Gemini, choosing the model for each bot, then sell them three ways: access through a paywall, resale rights through Multi-sell or outright through Transfer. Bots embed on any web page, including as a live FAQ assistant on a sales page. Every sale pays one published platform fee. Five tiers run from $5 to $297 a month. Selling in the Bot Market starts at Pro.

## Product Facts

- Models: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini at launch. The Botter chooses the model for each bot. Nothing is routed automatically.
- Three ways to sell: access through a per-bot paywall, resale rights through Multi-sell (copies sold with resale rights) and outright sale through Transfer (full ownership moves to the buyer and the seller loses access). Bot Market selling starts at Pro.
- Platform fee on every sale (paywall access purchases, message top-ups and Bot Market sales): 5% of the price after tax on Apprentice, Novice and Expert or 3% on Pro and Master, plus 30 cents, capped at the price after tax.
- Tiers: Apprentice $5 (1 bot, 300 boosts each month), Novice $47 (20 bots, 2,000 boosts), Expert $97 (50 bots, 5,000 boosts), Pro $197 (unlimited bots, 12,000 boosts), Master $297 (unlimited bots, 20,000 boosts). One boost is worth 1 cent at retail.
- Bot Market affiliate shares: sellers set an affiliate share on each listing. Any member from Novice up can promote it with their own link and earn that share of the price after the platform fee. Single level only.
- Botterverse affiliate programme: 50% of a referred member's first payment and 20% of every payment after, credited to the member's earnings and withdrawable to Stripe. Botter Boosters earn no commission. Rates can change, so say "at launch".
- Also included: an embeddable bot widget for any web page, an importer for moving an old GPT across and a data export, so Botters are never tied to one platform and can take their bots elsewhere if they want to.
- The member area is called the Botter's Lab.
- Paid features are not available in the EU because the complexity of EU tax rules means the service cannot be offered there. Mention this only in the FAQ and on the cart page. Never in headlines, body copy or emails.
- Billing: monthly or annual. Cancelling keeps access to the end of the current billing period. Monthly subscriptions are not refunded. Annual subscriptions refund the unused remainder of the year.
- Founders: 50% off for life on every plan with everything charged for real. The founders window is open during the beta and for two weeks after public launch. Permanent Founders badge on bot pages and no seat limit.
