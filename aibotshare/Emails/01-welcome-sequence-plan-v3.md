# Welcome sequence plan, version 3 (30 days and five tier tracks)

Draft 3 for review. Replaces `retired\01-welcome-sequence-plan-v2.md` (kept for reference). Nothing is set up in FluentCRM. The emails are written after you approve this plan.

## 1. How it works

- One email a day for 30 days. Every member receives 30 emails.
- Days 1 to 12 are the same for every tier.
- After day 12 the tiers branch off on different days. The lower the tier the earlier it branches. A tier stays on the shared emails for as long as the content suits it.
- Each tier then follows its own track. Apprentice and Novice and Expert tracks mix useful emails with upgrade emails. Pro has a few upgrade emails. Master has none.
- One tag controls the whole thing: **Welcome Active**. Remove it and the sequence stops. The member returns to the main list.
- Broadcast campaigns exclude anyone with Welcome Active so nobody receives a newsletter on top of a daily email.

## 2. Show, not tell

Every upgrade email shows what the member could achieve with one feature of a higher tier. It never lists benefits.

- A worked example the member can read in seconds: a sample screen or a sample conversation or a sample set of instructions.
- Any sample output comes from a real run. Any sample figure is labelled as an example.
- No income claims.
- One sentence at the end names the plan that has it and points to Change plan under Billing and boosts.
- No countdowns and no pressure.

## 3. Rules for every email

- Subject prefix: [Botterverse] for general emails and [Botters Lab] for emails about the Botters Lab. The welcome emails use [Botterverse] for S01 and [Botters Lab] for the feature emails. Sender name is Botterverse. No addresses or footers.
- One function and one bot (or none) and one thing to paste or click.
- Name the sign-in: Botters Lab (username and password) or billing login or help desk.
- Use the exact on-screen labels from the member guide.
- Never repeat the two links in the purchase email.
- No promised durations. No EU wording. No fixed refund or holdback figures. No Founder badge. No sixth plan.
- Say "customers who agree to share their details" whenever the CRM or customer list comes up.
- Remind people that preview and visitor messages use their boosts.
- Links to the Botters Lab use the domain token in section 9. Never a typed domain.
- Branded template: light and restrained. Plain background. One accent colour. One button. No banners or busy footers.

Details:
- Every email step waits one day. The first email of each chained automation waits one day after its trigger so the daily rhythm holds.
- A0 starts by applying Welcome Active and Customer. It removes Lead. Email 1 waits only a few minutes.
- Send time is the same every day. Use the Wait block set to the next chosen weekdays at a time of day. Choose every weekday and the same time.
- Plan tags exist one at a time. Plan filters use those tags.
- Broadcast campaigns exclude Welcome Active.
- The GPT variant: with the tag Migrated From GPT the member receives X1 on day 3 and S03 in the slot X1 would normally take. Nobody receives a gap.
- The nudges from version 2 are removed. A separate nudge email would break the one-a-day rhythm. The Support offer sits inside the daily emails instead.

## 8. Decision: what happens on an upgrade mid-sequence

Decided. An upgrade means the welcome sequence has worked. It cancels the member's track. The member then starts the upgrade sequence for the new tier. See `02-upgrade-sequences-plan.md`.

Stories open a few of the emails above. See `retired\03-storyselling-plan.md`.

## 9. Links and the domain

- Botters Lab sign-in is `https://lab.{{domain}}/login`. The token is the base domain only.
- In emails the same token is used. FluentCRM only parses codes written as group.key so the theme answers the `fluentcrm_smartcode_fallback` filter for `{{domain}}`. Links in emails must be absolute, for example `https://{{domain}}/pricing/`.
- The same technique applies everywhere a link would carry the domain. The site plan lists the places.
