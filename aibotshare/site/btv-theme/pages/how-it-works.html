From plan to live bot

Here is the whole journey in plain steps.

Choose a plan

Pick the plan on the pricing page. Buy with the email address you will register with.

Create your Botters Lab account

One email arrives after you buy. It has two links. The second one creates your account. Pick a username you like because it becomes part of your bots' web addresses.

Build your bot

Name it. Write what it should do in plain language. Add your files. Choose the model.

Preview it

Chat with it as a visitor would. Try a question it should refuse. Nobody else sees it yet.

Make it Public

Confirm the declarations. Press Make Public. Every bot has a safety scan before being approved.

Share it

Copy the share link or paste the embed into your own site.

Sell access

Put a one-time price on it. Set a message cap and a top-up. Add a money-back guarantee if you want one. This starts on Novice.

Watch it work

See conversations, credits and revenue on your dashboard. Deeper analytics start on Expert.

Three ways to reach people

Share a link

Every Public bot has its own page.

Put it on your site

One snippet.

List it on the Bot Market

Other Botters can buy it. Selling starts on Pro.

You stay in charge

  • You choose the model for each bot.
  • Your bots, knowledge and conversations go with you. Request an export from Settings.
  • Every bot is checked and shows what its owner declared on its Certified page.
