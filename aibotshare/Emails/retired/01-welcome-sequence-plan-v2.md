# Welcome sequence plan, version 2 (30 emails)

Draft 2 for review. Replaces the 12 email plan in `01-welcome-sequence-plan.md` (kept for reference). Nothing is set up in FluentCRM. The emails are written after you approve this plan.

## 1. Aim

Make the new member feel good about buying. Then introduce one function per email. Each email gives one bot to try and one thing to paste or click. A working bot exists within the first week.

## 2. How the one-bot rule works

- **Emails 3 to 10** build the same first bot, the **Quick Answer Bot**. Each email adds one function to it.
- **Later emails** give one new bot each. The bot shows off that email's function.
- Housekeeping emails have no bot.
- Every example bot comes with the text to paste. The member fills in a few blanks.

## 3. Voice

Playful and plain. The Botterverse words arrive gradually so the member is drawn in.

| Emails | Vocabulary |
|---|---|
| 1 to 4 | Botter and Botter's Lab only |
| 5 to 8 | Add Botting and light bot jokes |
| 9 to 17 | Introduce the Botterverse in email 9. Add fellow Botters and Bot Market |
| 18 to 30 | Full mix. Never forced. Never flowery |

Email 1 opens with "Congratulations on becoming our latest Botter". The subject line carries the same idea.

## 4. Rules for every email

- One function. One action. One thing to paste.
- Subject lines start with [Botterverse]. Sender name is Botterverse. No addresses or footers.
- Name the sign-in each time: Botter's Lab (username and password) or billing login or help desk.
- Use the exact on-screen labels from the member guide.
- Never repeat the two links in the purchase email.
- No promised durations. The guide gives none.
- Remind people that preview and visitor messages use their credits.
- A new bot is private until the member presses Make Public.
- No EU wording. No fixed refund or holdback figures. No Founder badge. No sixth plan.
- Only send a function to a plan that has it.
- Say "customers who agree to share their details" whenever the CRM or customer list comes up.
