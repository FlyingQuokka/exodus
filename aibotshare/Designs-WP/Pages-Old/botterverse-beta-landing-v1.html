The Bot Market

Three ways to turn one bot into income

Pick one or list a bot under all three. Selling in the Bot Market starts at Pro.

1

Sell access

The buyer pays to use your bot through a paywall. You keep ownership and set the price.

2

Sell resale rights

Multi-sell sells copies with RR, MRR or PLR style rights. The buyer can resell them, the way you already sell rights to an info product.

3

Sell it outright

Transfer hands full ownership to the buyer. You no longer have access. A clean, one-time exit for a bot you are done building.

FAQ

Questions this exact bot is answering right now

Everything below is answered live by an Botterverse bot embedded on this page. That is the embeddable-widget feature, working in front of you.

Will Botterverse pull the rug the way OpenAI did?
No platform can promise the future. What you do have is a choice of four models rather than one vendor, published fees rather than an undisclosed formula and a data export for what you build.
What does the platform take from my sales?
One published fee on every sale: 5% of the price after tax on Apprentice, Novice and Expert or 3% on Pro and Master, plus 30 cents. It covers paywall access purchases, message top-ups and Bot Market sales.
What are the three ways to sell a bot?
Sell access through a paywall; sell copies with resale rights through Multi-sell; or sell the bot outright through Transfer. Selling in the Bot Market starts at Pro.
Can I offer affiliates a share of my sales?
Yes, on Bot Market listings. You set the affiliate share on each listing. Any member from Novice up can promote it with their own link and earn that share of the price after the platform fee. Only one level is paid.
