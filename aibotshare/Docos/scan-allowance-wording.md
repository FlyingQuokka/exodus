# Monthly safety scan allowance: wording for the owner and the marketing agent

The owner's decision of 2 November 2026. Nothing here is published. Each piece is for a person to approve and put in place.

## The terms

**How the terms are held.** Each version of the terms is one `document_version` row of type `terms`. The one in force is what members read. `resources/documents/terms.md` only seeds version 1 on a new database, so changing that file changes nothing on the live site. A saved version can never be edited.

**The exact place to change them.** Back Office, **Trust and safety**, **Legal documents**, **Add a version**, with the **Type** set to **Terms and conditions**. The **Full text** fills with the version in force. Add the wording below to it, set **Takes effect** and press **Save**. The staff guide's section 3.8 has the procedure.

**What saving does.** Every member who accepted the terms is emailed and must accept the new version before they next publish or list a bot. So choose when to do it. If the WordPress site carries its own copy of the terms, that page needs the same change (staff guide section 4.5). No version has been published for this work.

**Where it goes.** At the end of section 5, "Credits and what you pay", just before the heading "Auto top-up".

**The wording for the owner to approve:**

> **Safety scans.** Your plan includes a number of safety scans each calendar month, counted in Coordinated Universal Time. The number for each plan is shown on our pricing page. A safety scan counts when you started it and our screening gave a result, even where the result is that your bot is refused. A scan that could not be finished does not count and neither does one we start ourselves.
>
> Each safety scan beyond your plan's number uses credits, charged on what the scan actually cost us at your plan's rate. The count starts again each month and nothing carries over.
>
> Once your included safety scans are used, we may refuse to make a bot public or to submit a listing while your credits are too low to cover another scan. We always screen a saved change to a public bot, however few credits you have. The charge for a safety scan takes what your balance holds and stops at zero. You owe us nothing further for that scan.

The rest of section 5 needs no change. "Credits pay for the model calls your bots make" stays true, since a scan is a model call made for your bot.

## Wording for the marketing agent

**What is included per plan:**

| Plan | Safety scans included per month |
|---|---|
| Apprentice | 5 |
| Novice | 40 |
| Expert | 100 |
| Pro | 250 |
| Master | 350 |

Empire also has 350, for when it goes on sale.

**What a safety scan is.** Every bot is checked before it goes Public, on every saved change to a Public bot and when it is listed on Bot Market. Each check is one safety scan. Saving licence terms is one too.

**What happens beyond it.** Most members never reach their number. Those who do pay for each further scan in credits, on what the scan really cost at their plan's credit rate. The count starts again each month and nothing carries over. A change to a Public bot is always checked, so a bot is never left unchecked for want of credits. A charge always stops at zero. A new publish waits until the member has enough credits for a typical scan.

**A short pricing page line:**

> Safety scans included every month. Most members never reach their number. Beyond it, each scan simply uses credits.

**Please keep to these points.** Use the figures above and no cost figures. Say included credits "reset", never "expire". Say "Botter Support Team", never "staff" or "the team".
