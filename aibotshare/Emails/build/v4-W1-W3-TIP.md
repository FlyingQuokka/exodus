## W1 - Put your bot on your website (day 6, all tiers)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] Put your bot on your own website

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: last time we covered credits, Auto top-up and promo codes, which pay for every message your bot sends. We said your bot would move onto your own website next.

New topic: the Embed snippet is a small piece of code that puts your bot on a page you control. It takes a few minutes once your bot is Public.

**Adding the snippet**

Open the bot from [Bot list](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots) and go to its [Create a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/create) page. Sharing and embed only works once the bot is saved and a Personal bot has no embed at all.

1. In Sharing and embed, press the copy button beside Embed snippet.
2. Paste it into your website's custom code box or HTML box.
3. Save the page and open it to check the bot appears.

The embed follows the bot, so later changes show up without pasting again. Visitors are told they're talking to an AI assistant before the first reply.

**Why you may see a button and not a chat**

Require visitors to sign up is on for a new bot. While it's on, the embed shows a button that says "Chat with" followed by your bot's name. The button opens your bot's page in a new tab.

Switch it off in Sharing and embed and a free bot runs its chat inside your page. A paid bot always needs an account, so it keeps the button.

**Set a Daily credit limit first**

With sign-up off, anyone can chat at once and every message uses your credits. In Sharing and embed, Daily credit limit lets you leave the box empty to use the platform default or enter a number of whole credits.

When a free visitor reaches the limit, they're told the bot has reached its limit for the day while paid buyers and you carry on. It resets at midnight UTC and we email you once that day.

**Task for today**

Paste the Embed snippet into one page of your site. In Sharing and embed, decide whether Require visitors to sign up stays on, then set a Daily credit limit and press Save.

See you tomorrow,

Your Botter Support Team

P.S. Next up: which kinds of bot work well on a website.

Require visitors to sign up is on for a new bot so the embed shows a "Chat with" button that opens the bot's page in a new tab. Off for a free bot runs the chat in the page. ## W2 - Website bots for a sales page (day 7, all tiers)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] Bots that work well on a website

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday you put the Embed snippet on a page and set a Daily credit limit. We left one question open: which kinds of bot work well on a website.

New topic: two bots suit a sales page well. An FAQ bot answers questions about a product or an offer and a product helper suggests which product or package fits.

Both follow one rule. They answer only from your own material and they never make income or results claims.

**An FAQ bot**

Build it on the [Create a bot](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/create) page. Everything below is for example only, so swap in your own details.

| Field | For example |
|---|---|
| Name (in Details) | Ask about [your product name] |
| Description (in Details) | Answers questions about [your product name] using the information on this page. |
| Category | Customer Support. Choose from the Category list or press Suggest a category |
| Instructions (in What the bot should do) | You answer questions from visitors to the sales page for [your product name]. Use only the knowledge files and keep answers short and polite.<br>If the answer isn't in the files say you don't know and point to [your contact page].<br>Never promise results or earnings. Don't discuss anything unrelated to [your product name]. |
| Conversation starters | What's included?<br>How does delivery work?<br>What is your refund policy? |
| Knowledge file contents (faq.md) | One heading per question with your answer written exactly as you would say it. Cover what's included and who it's for and how delivery works and your refund terms and how to contact you. |

**A product helper**

| Field | For example |
|---|---|
| Name (in Details) | Which [your product name] fits me? |
| Description (in Details) | Asks a few questions then suggests the package that fits best. |
| Category | Sales. Choose from the Category list or press Suggest a category |
| Instructions (in What the bot should do) | You help visitors choose between the packages in the knowledge file. Ask up to three short questions about what they need then recommend one package and explain why using only the file.<br>If none fits say so plainly. Never promise results or earnings. |
| Conversation starters | Which option suits me?<br>What's the difference between the packages?<br>I'm new to this - where do I start? |
| Knowledge file contents (packages.md) | One section per package with its name and who it's for and what's included and what isn't and where to buy. |

**Task for today**

Pick one of the two and build it from your own sales page. Save the bot first, then in Knowledge files, press Add a file and wait for the status to read Ready.

Try three real questions in Preview before you embed it. Preview uses your credits.

Happy building,

Your Botter Support Team

- Facts: Create a bot sections and exact names (Details and Category and Instructions with the box What the bot should do and Conversation starters and Knowledge files). Suggest a category uses no credits. Knowledge files accept PDF and Word and text and Markdown and CSV and only work once the bot is saved. Status goes Being read then Ready and only ready files are used. Preview uses the owner's credits (3.1 to 3.9).

## TIP1 - Know what a message costs (day 17, all tiers)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] What one message costs

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: last time we read the numbers for a bot. We left one question open: what one message costs.

New topic: every message uses credits by its real size, so it's worth knowing roughly what a message costs before you set a price for access.

**How to find out**

Note your Credit balance on the [Dashboard](https://lab.{{domain}}/), then send ten typical questions to your bot in Preview. Check the Credit balance again and divide the difference by ten.

Balances show in whole credits and round down, so ten messages give a steadier figure than one. If your plan includes [Analytics](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots/analytics), the basic panel also shows credits used and credits per message.

**Three things that change the figure**

- A dearer model uses more credits per message. You choose it in Model.
- Testing in Preview uses credits too, so your own tests count.
- Longer questions and longer answers use more.

If you sell access with a paywall, each buyer message is paid from your credits. A Message cap is there to protect you, so work out the cost per message first and then decide on a One-time price and a cap.

**Task for today**

Run the ten-message test on your busiest bot and write the result down.

Happy testing,

Your Botter Support Team

P.S. Next up: finding your busy hours.

**Notes**
- Facts: each message uses credits by its real size and a more expensive model uses more. Preview uses the owner's credits. Dashboard shows Credit balance in whole credits (rounded down). Basic analytics (credits used and credits per message) is Novice Botter and above. The paywall and Message cap are Novice Botter and above. The Message cap protects the owner because each buyer message is paid from the owner's credits (3.8 and 3.9 and 5.3 and 8.1 and 10.1 and 10.2).
- The paywall is described and not instructed so Apprentice members are not told to switch it on.
- Longer questions and answers use more credits: the guide says credits are by "real size".
- No prices or credit amounts. Pricing is not linked because no figure is given.

## TIP2 - A free bot as a teaser (day 28, all tiers)

**Subject:** [Botters Lab] A free bot as a teaser

**Body:**

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Recap: yesterday's example was a bot for a newsletter or community. We left one idea open: a free bot as a teaser.

New topic: a free Public bot can show visitors what you know and a paid bot can hold the full version. Many Botters build both.

**How the pair works**

The teaser answers your most common questions from a short knowledge file. The full version draws on your complete material and sits behind a paywall with a One-time price.

A paywall belongs to Novice Botter and above. On Apprentice Botter, the free bot still works as it stands because you can share it by link or embed.

To build the second bot, open the first from [Bot list](https://lab.{{domain}}/bots) and press Duplicate. The copy starts Personal with its paywall off and keeps the instructions, files, starters and model.

A duplicate counts towards your plan's bot limit like any other bot.

**Study Browse**

[Browse](https://lab.{{domain}}/market) shows how other Botters describe their bots. Use Search Bot Market and the 4 stars and up filter or sort by Top rated.

Read the name, description and category of five bots. Notice which descriptions make clear what a visitor receives.

**Task for today**

Write down what a visitor would learn from your free bot and what stays in the full version. Then open Browse and read five descriptions.
