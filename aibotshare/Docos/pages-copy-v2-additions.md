## 3. Bot Market page

**Page title:** Bot Market

**Heading:** Buy a bot. Or sell one you built.

**Sub:** Members sell bots to each other. The buyer receives the bot itself and runs it on their own credits.

**Button:** Take Me To The Botterverse

### Who can do what

- **Buy.** Every plan can buy.
- **Sell.** Selling starts on Pro.

### Two ways to sell

- **Transfer.** Ownership moves to the buyer. The bot sells once.
- **Multi-sell.** Each buyer receives their own copy. The seller keeps the original.

### Four licences

- **Personal use.** The buyer uses the bot themselves. They cannot make it Public, sell access to it, list it or edit it.
- **Resell rights.** The buyer can make it Public and sell access to it. They can sell copies on Personal use. They cannot edit or rebrand it.
- **Master resell rights.** As Resell rights but the buyer can sell copies on Personal use or Resell rights.
- **PLR.** The buyer can edit and rebrand it. They can sell it on Personal use, Resell rights or Master resell rights.

[Checked against the licence table in the member guide on 1 November 2026.]

### How to browse and buy

1. Open **Browse** in the Botter's Lab.
2. Search or filter by category, model, sale type and rating.
3. Open a listing. Read the reviews and the seller's profile.
4. Press **Buy**. Read the licence you are buying and accept the seller's terms.
5. Pay. The bot appears in your Bot list marked **Purchased**.

Before you use a bought bot with customers enter any integration credentials and confirm its declarations. Integration passwords and keys never move with a bot.

### What a seller does

1. Choose a Public bot.
2. Press **List a bot for sale** in My listings.
3. Write the description. Botterverse can suggest one.
4. Choose the sale type and the licence.
5. Set the price.
6. Set an optional affiliate share. Members from Novice up can then promote your listing.
7. Submit it for review.

Sellers save their own licence terms in Settings. Botterverse gives no legal advice about them.

### Every listing is open about the bot

Each listing carries a **Certified by Botterverse** page. It shows who owns the bot, the model it runs on and what the owner has declared. It does not vouch for the answers. Every listing also has **Report this bot**. Anyone can use it without an account.

### Refunds

If a bot you bought does not work or is not as described you can ask for a refund. The Botter Support Team decides. It decides in those two cases only.
