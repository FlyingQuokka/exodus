## 3. How it works

**Page title:** How it works

**Heading:** From plan to live bot

**Sub:** Here is the whole journey in plain steps.

**1. Choose a plan**
Pick the plan on the pricing page. Buy with the email address you will register with.

**2. Create your Botters Lab account**
One email arrives after you buy. It has two links. The second one creates your account. Pick a username you like because it becomes part of your bots' web addresses.

**3. Build your bot**
Name it. Write what it should do in plain language. Add your files. Choose the model.

**4. Preview it**
Chat with it as a visitor would. Try a question it should refuse. Nobody else sees it yet.

**5. Make it Public**
Confirm the declarations. Press Make Public. Every bot is checked first.

**6. Share it**
Copy the share link or paste the embed into your own site.

**7. Sell access**
Put a one-time price on it. Set a message cap and a top-up. Add a money-back guarantee if you want one. This starts on Novice.

**8. Watch it work**
See conversations, credits and revenue on your dashboard. Deeper analytics start on Expert.

### Three ways to reach people

- **Share a link.** Every Public bot has its own page.
- **Put it on your site.** One snippet.
- **List it on Bot Market.** Other Botters can buy it. Selling starts on Pro.

### You stay in charge

- You choose the model for each bot.
- Your bots, knowledge and conversations go with you. Request an export from Settings.
- Every bot is checked and shows what its owner declared on its Certified page.

## 4. FAQ

**Page title:** Questions

[Ten questions that answer the main objections. The other 20 from draft 1 move to the help articles. The home page repeats the first six. The Founders answer is dropped because Founders are not mentioned in copy.]

**Do I need to code?**
No. You write instructions in plain language and upload files. Integrations use a form.

**Is there a free plan?**
No. The cheapest plan is on the [Pricing](/pricing/) page. Visitors can still chat with free Public bots without paying.

**Who pays when people chat with my bot?**
You do. Each message uses credits from your balance. Your plan includes credits each month and you can buy more. Set a daily credit limit on a bot and a message cap on a paid bot so a busy day cannot use up your balance.

**Can I move my custom GPT?**
Yes. Paste its instructions into Import a bot and upload its files. It does not connect to your OpenAI account so you paste the content in. Rebuild any actions as integrations from Novice. The last step lists anything that could not be carried across. It uses no credits.

**Which AI models can I use?**
Claude (the default), GPT, Gemini or Grok. You choose for each bot. Apprentice has the everyday models. Every other plan has every model.

**Will I be tied to Botterverse?**
No. Request an export from Settings. Your bots, knowledge and conversations go with you. You can close your account at any time.

**Can I sell access to my bots?**
Yes. Charge for access with a paywall from Novice. List a bot on Bot Market from Pro. Botterverse takes one fee on each sale: 5% of the price after tax plus 30 cents on Novice and Expert. From Pro it is 3% plus 30 cents. For example a $49 sale on Novice carries a fee of $2.45 plus $0.30 so your earnings from it are $46.25. That is arithmetic and not an earnings claim.

**When can I withdraw my earnings?**
A sale can be refunded for 14 days. Earnings from a sale become withdrawable 21 days after it. The smallest withdrawal is $50. Request a withdrawal in the Botters Lab. Payout fees are passed on at cost and shown before you confirm. Some countries cannot be paid yet.

**Can I change plan or cancel?**
Yes to both. An upgrade is prorated. If you move down any bots over the new limit are paused and you choose which keep running. A monthly plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. After a plan ends your account stays open. Your bots pause and your data is kept.

**Can I use Botterverse in the EU?**
You can use free Public bots. We cannot offer our paid services in the EU because the complexity of the EU tax rules makes it impractical for a small business to comply. That covers plans, credits, bot access and Bot Market purchases.
