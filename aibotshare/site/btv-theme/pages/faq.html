FAQ

Questions

Short answers to the questions people ask most.

Do I need to code?

No. You write instructions in plain language and upload files. Integrations use a form.

Is there a free plan?

No. The cheapest plan is on the Pricing page. Visitors can still chat with free Public bots without paying.

Who pays when people chat with my bot?

You do. Each message uses credits from your balance. Your plan includes credits each month. You can buy more. Set a daily credit limit on a bot and a message cap on a paid bot so a busy day cannot use up your balance.

Can I move my custom GPT?

Yes. Paste its instructions into Import a bot and upload its files. It does not connect to your OpenAI account so you paste the content in. Rebuild any actions as integrations from Novice. The last step lists anything that could not be carried across. It uses no credits.

Which AI models can I use?

Claude (the default), GPT, Gemini or Grok. You choose for each bot. Apprentice has the everyday models. Every other plan has every model.

Will I be tied to Botterverse?

No. Request an export from Settings. Your bots, knowledge and conversations go with you. You can close your account at any time.

Can I sell access to my bots?

Yes. Charge for access with a paywall from Novice. List a bot on Bot Market from Pro. Botterverse takes one fee on each sale: 5% of the price after tax plus 30 cents on Novice and Expert. From Pro it is 3% plus 30 cents. For example a $49 sale on Novice carries a fee of $2.45 plus $0.30 so your earnings from it are $46.25. That is arithmetic and not an earnings claim.

When can I withdraw my earnings?

A sale can be refunded for 14 days. Earnings from a sale become withdrawable 21 days after it. The smallest withdrawal is $50. Request a withdrawal in the Botters Lab. Payout fees are passed on at cost and shown before you confirm. Some countries cannot be paid yet.

Can I change plan or cancel?

Yes to both. An upgrade is prorated. If you move down any bots over the new limit are paused. You choose which keep running. A monthly plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. After a plan ends your account stays open. Your bots pause. Your data is kept.

Can I use Botterverse in the EU?

You can use free Public bots. We cannot offer our paid services in the EU because the complexity of the EU tax rules makes it impractical for a small business to comply. That covers plans, credits, bot access and Bot Market purchases.
